Official public data version 10.6084/m9.figshare.26412295.v4, species cover and environmental files as described in article data-availability statement. Acquire exact version using public Figshare API, file-size and MD5 checks, SHA256 receipts, original archive only as Actions artifact. Verify actual site, treatment, plot, block, calendar and treatment-year keys, baseline before addition, zero vs missing, species-level vs genus aggregates. Do not assume published 72 sites or data layout means usable European coverage. No new population observations or source-specific wastewater attribution. Original article already examined plant alpha/gamma/beta diversity; distinct analysis here concerns potential larval resource cover and identity, not repeating its main claim.

Hypothesis: even if nutrient enrichment reduces plant richness, the cover of documented butterfly host plants may increase or decrease, and breadth of potential hosts and amount of cover need not change in the same direction. Only test regional butterfly host catalogues in Europe; do not project European butterfly species into global grasslands. Exact accepted plant name joins to fixed prior 2 catalogues and scored vascular key reference; no genus-aggregate imputation, keep unknown coverage. Rebuild old/Clarke sources to match prior 1025/1974 accepted edges and common 94 eligible keys. Source gate: at least 10 European sites, at least 3 countries and 3 paired blocks per site, both unfenced control and full NPK, untreated baseline (year 0) and treatment year 4 observed for same plots. Fixed common consumer roster selected from source taxonomy/known geographical Europe, not outcomes; at least 70% scored plant cover and 3 exact taxa per plot in both times and treatments; report coverage by treatment, no post-outcome threshold relaxation. If data do not contain baseline or plot/block identity or adequate European coverage, STOP. If it passes, calculate site-equal difference-in-differences for mean known potential host cover (mean summed cover/known scored host count across fixed common butterflies), number of recorded known hosts, per-butterfly potential host identity turnover, and plant richness comparator. Both catalogues must agree for main interpretation; within-block randomization/shuffled labels only if original design confirmed, whole-site bootstrap, no treating species, blocks or years as independent sites. Plant cover is not biomass, nutritional assimilation, realized feeding or actual consumer populations. Combined NPK does not isolate N/P effects or establish sewage effect.
