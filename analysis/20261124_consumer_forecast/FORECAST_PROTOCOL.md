## Protocol frozen before opening third-period species counts
This is a historical-model temporal and geographic holdout, not causal evidence or a truly prospective field experiment. First train only 2017–19 predictors -> 2020–22 butterfly response, on original quality 173 H7 with each target H6 excluded; preserve original 5 fold assignments. Apply unchanged training transformations and coefficients to 2020–22 predictors and save predictions BEFORE downloading 2023–25 species records. The response is species-specific probability of inclusion among 20 pooled five-family indexed butterfly records in the NEXT period. Denominator includes all identified keys; targets fixed to previously recorded accepted mapped keys. It contains no plant terms and does not equal abundance, occupancy or realized interaction. Baseline includes prior same-species inclusion, family inclusion mass, species indicators, source EQR (2018 or 2022), static landscape/WWTP geography, log indexed butterfly/plant counts, plant S50 and summed host inclusion in the fixed 1176 host-key union. Add mean of 50-record inclusion probabilities for catalogue hosts (degree-normalized). Same-parameter alternative uses exact mean shuffled graph preserving family × training-early frequency × geographical cluster. All graph and scaling estimates use training 2017–19 only. Ridge alpha=10, equal H6 weights, fixed clipping to [0,1], independent closed-form solver check. Persistence is separate comparator. No fitting on 2023–25. Source support gate only controls score eligibility, not training.
Primary common Lycaenidae comparison: original 46 accepted species shared by Ferrer-Paris and restricted Clarke evidence1–3, expanded Clarke 1176-key union, prior two-period common support >=80% among accepted mapped Lycaenidae. Both graphs on identical taxa and sites. Five families separately with original catalogue, exploratory, compare gains not biological effect sizes. Do not treat a stronger point estimate as significant between-family difference. Scores H6-equal MSE reductions with 5000 fixed-loss H6 bootstrap; primary support requires both common-catalogue graphs improve over baseline AND structured null on quality panel, intervals >0; this intersection rule does not rescue individual negatives. All eligible cohort and persistence comparisons are sensitivity/descriptive; no tuning. Minimum 40 H7 and 20 H6 per evaluation. Report known-key share of new records; fixed key match is not comprehensive taxonomy revalidation, which must be completed before biological claims. New keys are not dropped from denominators.
This answers whether known resource composition predicts later consumer record composition. It does NOT identify whether a *change* in resource composition precedes a change in consumer composition independently of persistence; a genuine historical training analogue of that two-interval lag requires a fourth period or a different spatial validation estimand. Do not mislabel this as that stronger test. No wastewater causal effect, no 2024 EQR fit. Third-period source previously acquired but not inspected for outcomes during specification. No claim of new ecological result until scoring and source audit succeed.
