Evidence: Proven=1 (400 rows), Larvae=2 (2848), Eggs=3 (271), Claim only=4 (1821), Breeding only=5 (154), Mistake=6 (95). Exclude 4–6 and rows without a non-questionable country. The source is European catalogue evidence (egg/oviposition is not proof of successful feeding). Accepted butterfly binomials are matched to exact canonical names or unique original verified GBIF aliases. Eight GBIF keys with multiple accepted Clarke names are excluded rather than collapsing species. Plants are restricted to 990 original host keys using exact species-level verified original taxonomic crosswalk and unambiguous binomial names; reject rank markers, hybrids, ambiguous source names, and family disagreements. No fuzzy matching or newly inferred host edges. Unique accepted binomials are not a definitive reconciliation of taxon concepts; crosswalk/authority disagreements can remain. Record geography is screened for any non-questionable country, not matched to exact H7 sites; the evidence label is the source's pooled relationship-level label. The 5,589-row mapping disposition is retained (gzip) and 256 reliable mapped links across 52 butterfly keys and 87 plants are saved. No actual local feeding or abundance is inferred.

## Frozen common-support comparison

Same 454 original H7-periods, same fixed 990 plant universe and 20/50 record rarefaction; restrict to accepted butterfly keys with a reliable edge in both catalogues; 80% of original mapped Lycaenidae inclusion mass represented in both periods. Both evidence cutoffs pass fixed minimum30 common species, 60 host plants per graph, 40 paired H7/20 H6. Level1–2: 45 keys, old203 plants and262 edges, new84 plants and229 edges, shared93, same cohort sizes. Separate exact permutation-mean reference for each graph; strata built exclusively from early training H6, no held-out H6 or late observations. Same folds, H6-equal ridge alpha10, early network value and covariates; 5,000 paired H6 fixed-loss resamples. Fixed graph targets differ across catalogues, so gains are predictive diagnostics, not commensurate biological effect sizes. Not an equivalence test.
