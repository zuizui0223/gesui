# Clarke 2024 catalogue sensitivity: source-first protocol

Frozen after the existing Ferrer-Paris results, before the Clarke numerical ecological results. Not an independent field replication; reference literature may overlap. DOI 10.5061/dryad.1vhhmgr12; article 10.1002/ece3.10834.

## Source gate
Retrieve published Appendix S1 or the original Dryad tables. Original Dryad v274312 inventory in signed host-source artifact 14114436781 fixes exact SHA256 for all four CSVs. A schema-only pass is insufficient: verify downloaded bytes against that inventory before claiming a Dryad original. For Appendix S1, verify provenance, sheet/header, evidence coding and record counts against article; preserve bytes/hash. Do not substitute scraped narrative, generated records, or infer undocumented links from plant family. Keep reliable field use (levels 1–3), exclude questionable-only geographical records where source supplies this distinction. Level 1–2 sensitivity. Exclude taxonomic genus-only, infraspecific, ambiguous and nonaccepted mappings, no fuzzy matching. Use exact accepted binomial and unique verified synonyms only, report mapping stages separately; retain missing catalogue information as unknown.

## Primary comparison
Same original 454 H7-period panel, 222 paired H7, quality 173 H7/77 H6. Compare Ferrer-Paris and Clarke on identical source-observed butterfly keys (accepted Lycaenidae, at least one reliable matched host in each catalogue), identical fixed 990 original mapped plant-key universe, original 20 butterfly/50 plant record inclusion probabilities, same dates, paired H7 and H6 fold assignment. This deliberately tests alternate edges on common support, NOT comprehensive additional Clarke host discoveries. Report full original and matched old reference separately. Source-only gate before modeling: at least 30 common Lycaenidae keys, 60 distinct supported plant keys in each graph, 40 paired H7 in 20 H6, and at least 80% of original host-mapped Lycaenidae inclusion mass represented by shared taxa in both periods (per H7). Report all attrition; no relaxation after looking at results. Strict level 1–2 may STOP.

Rebuild the exact reference mean separately within the existing early-training H6-only strata (plant family, occurrence frequency, early spatial clusters; no held-out H6 or late data). Same early covariates, country/method categories, H6-equal ridge alpha 10, fixed H6-paired loss bootstrap. Primary plant symmetric change and paired alternate-catalogue contrast; secondary early-consumer-fixed plant component and consumer symmetric change. No empirical claim of equivalent effects from a nonsignificant difference; retain signs, intervals and failures. Compare cross-catalogue edge overlap on identical taxon support. Do not combine predictive improvements across different estimands as a meta-analysis. Country transfer and FR73 measured TP are additional tests only if matching gate passes. No new causal wastewater, actual host use, biomass, competition or population claim.

A source STOP means source unavailable or support insufficient, not biological absence. Do not fit numerical models to incomplete acquisitions. All positive, negative and STOP results, hashes, code and source receipts should be saved to PR #153; do not change main.
