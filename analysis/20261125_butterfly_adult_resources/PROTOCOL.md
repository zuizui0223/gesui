# Butterfly adult non-floral resources: source gate and biological question (2026-11-25)
New work builds on PR20 and PR21–43 as well as PR95/105/134/153/156/160/161, without merging any main branch. The initial observed Lycaenidae photo share association is not independently replicated by the Denmark standardized counts; all previous negative controls, denominator reversal, prior-state sensitivity and host-catalogue failures stand.

Biological hypothesis: landscape enrichment may reorganize larval host opportunities and adult non-floral feeding opportunities differently; this may produce within-family or within-genus contrasts not explained by larval host identity or nutrient-affinity alone. A real decoupling requires separate measurements of adult resource availability/use and larval resources. A species trait is not a local feeding event or a dose. Literature motivates substrate-specific, not universal sodium or protein assumptions: Beck et al. 1999 doi:10.1007/s004420050770 (Borneo); Boggs & Dau 2004 doi:10.1603/0046-225X-33.4.1020; 2025 review doi:10.1093/aesa/saaf007. Larval host niche and adult nectar niche comparison already exists (Altermatt & Pearse 2011 doi:10.1086/661248). Do not claim the concept of multiple adult/larval resources is novel.
