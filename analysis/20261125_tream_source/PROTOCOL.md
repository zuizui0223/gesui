# TREAM independent-observation opportunity: source-only freeze
2026-11-25, after all prior GBIF and ChemPop results. This step acquires the exact publisher-deposited TREAM data package (Welti & Haase, DOI 10.5063/F1NG4P4R; data descriptor 10.1038/s41597-024-03445-3). Published 1816 site series/22 countries/41 projects, 1968–2020. The 2023 Nature analysis uses the same TREAM observations, not an independent validation of that paper. No prior TREAM use found in search of gesui PR metadata; not exhaustive repository audit. No novel biodiversity effect or wastewater attribution claimed.

Purpose: test whether the previous space/time and taxonomic-resolution pattern extends to repeated standardized river community observations, rather than opportunistic records. Only source feasibility in this PR. TREAM includes annual climate and urban/crop cover (land cover 1992–2018); **not a directly measured sewage N/P or annual receiving-water chemistry panel**. A landscape association is not a wastewater effect. Do not replace missing chemistry with urban land cover.

Source gates: resolve publisher DOI through documented DataONE/KNB interfaces. Retrieve EML metadata, exact 3 named CSV data objects TREAM_siteLevel.csv, TREAM_siteYEARLevel.csv and TREAM_alltaxa.csv from public publisher-disclosed URLs; record byte SHA256 and publisher checksum when available. No bypass of authentication or rehosting raw data. Inspect original headers and metadata-defined attributes. Never select or fit on abundance, biodiversity metric or effect values in this stage. Stream only site/year and available taxonomic label keys; site-level country, project, stated taxonomy and method, and environmental coverage fields if unambiguous. On schema ambiguity retain header and STOP for review, do not silently guess. Require unique native site IDs and site-year keys, exact matching of taxa site-years to site-year table (missing documented cases reported), explicit country/project and order/family coverage, no imputation. Check published duplicate sites 100000001/100000309 and 100000002/100000308 and documented Danish changing taxonomic identification. Retain duplicates in raw census, exclude both pairs from proposed primary panel. Primary conservative opportunity excludes UK and France (known prior ChemPop and France panels; other EEA site overlap still to audit). Do not claim field independence before site/source checks. Proposed fixed windows 2007–09, 2010–12, 2013–15, 2016–18, chosen to stay within documented annual land-cover availability, require >=2 distinct sampled years in each window and no method/taxonomy changes when reported. Source-only target >=100 sites, >=5 countries, >=10 projects and >=20 sites in at least 3 countries for cross-project/country comparison. Report counts on all and conservative candidate panels, no threshold relaxation after results. These are opportunity thresholds, not proof of taxonomic or environmental quality. Environmental nonmissing coverage, original data semantics, physical site overlap and taxonomy-level stability require subsequent audit before fitting.

Future design (not yet executed): aggregate identical sampled visits to family/order incidence for fine/coarse composition (only source-documented taxonomy; do not invent genus/species when recorded as family). Use matched calendar windows, cross-time geographic persistence and identical-grain change associations with annual climate and land cover; within-project standardization, held-out project/country and temporal split. Compare genuine finer-level label information to matched nested nulls only if source supports it. Standardized within-series methodology differs between projects. Do not compare numerical effect sizes directly to GBIF, EQR, BMWP or US EPA data. No main or existing draft merge.
