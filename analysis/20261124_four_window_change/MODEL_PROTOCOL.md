It asks whether signed resource change is more informative conditional on coherence beyond its mean and magnitude. Common coherence support across both catalogues within each training interval; scoring requires identifiable C for both graphs and both historical predictor intervals. This avoids conditioning historical training on later plant data; train and score support definitions differ as explicitly declared here before fitting. No imputing undefined C; report STOP.
