# First real gesui all-Plantae GBIF index: source-frozen exploratory null

Original successful real source run: https://github.com/zuizui0223/gesui/actions/runs/42364079195

Model HydroWASTE outfall vs WWTP site; a 2-km radius GBIF 2011–2024 Plantae speciesKey index, with no butterfly-host filtering. The facilities were selected from source data BEFORE requesting any plant response.

- 58,502 WWTP records source-verified; 3,518 qualify after source-only geography/status/flow/quality gates.
- Five high/low source-fraction country pairs (AUT,CHE,CZE,DEU,DNK), 10 WWTPs, 20 GBIF survey points.
- Nine facility comparisons have both sites sufficiently recorded; four high/low country pairs complete.
- Effluent-fraction proxy vs outfall-minus-WWTP, rarefied to 30 species-keyed GBIF records: rho = -0.150, descriptive p = 0.7001 (n=9).
- Mean high-minus-low country-pair outfall/WWTP contrast = -0.0831 log richness (4 country pairs); all four contrasts negative.
- This is NOT an actual hydrologic upstream/downstream test. WWTP and outfall points represent different landscapes and recording regimes, not a validated counterfactual. This pilot does not show an N/P mechanism or causal plant diversity decline.
- The CZE high outfall site had only 11 indexed plant records and failed the minimum 30-record gate.

Raw source-lock sample design, per-site GBIF speciesKey counts, per-facility outcomes, and exact-byte hashes are included alongside this report. The original chocho GEB positive results and previously measured positive WWp/port-index correlations remain independent; no contradiction is implied.
