# Independent year 2018 replication: actual WWTP measured P/N and official aquatic EQR

The entire source-only 2018 sampling unit and 5-fold geographic split were committed BEFORE opening 2018 biological EQR. Do not substitute 2022 WWTP loads. EEA UWWTD Waterbase 2017-2018 v07/r01 original public archive and EEA Biology2026 2018 normalized EQR are exact source versions.

## Actual 2018 temporal validation

Benthic invertebrate source-only 2,639 river sites (204 with registered 2018 measured source) yielded 2,634 original valid EQR (202 exposed) from 15 countries and 52 spatial blocks. Five 3-degree source-only folds, Ridge(alpha=10), baseline geography, riverQ, upstream area, country and biological procedure method.

| 2018 original benthic model | Baseline MSE | Add UPSTREAM measured annual load MSE | Add DOWNSTREAM load placebo MSE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| P | 0.0529148 | 0.0523954 | 0.0527494 |
| N | 0.0529148 | 0.0522850 | 0.0528980 |

Original year-2018 benthic prediction MSE improvement from including upstream P: **+0.9816%**, upstream N **+1.1902%**. BOTH improve on baseline AND outperform physically downstream source placebo; **the preregistered independent-temporal-prediction direction was reproduced**. Source mass vs EQR raw Spearman P **-0.1314**, N **-0.1320**; this is not evidence discharge is beneficial to biodiversity.

2018 phytobenthos 2,670 EQR sites (220 source-exposed) has only P **+0.0085%** and N **+0.1254%** MSE improvements; the DOWNSTREAM negative-control predictor outperforms the upstream predictor in both nutrients, so 2018 predeclared specificity criterion **FAILS**. Fish only 2 original valid 2018 source-exposed sites across two reporting countries; original sample gate FAILED and no fish EQR model fitted.

Comparison with original 2022 source-year: benthic upstream-load MSE improvement P **+1.10%**, N **+1.40%**. 2018 uses different actual EEA original treatment/discharge reports, same type of standardized 2026 Biology EQR but independent 2018 records and source-only region/fold definitions. This is **a meaningful two-reference-year positive prediction**, not a fully independent sampling agency or randomized experiment.

## Independent reproducibility and causal limits

No external biological source was queried in the independent audit; it checks source CRC/SHA, all original source-only 2018 IDs, exact historic WWTP 2018 annual N/P masses, regional heldout folds, all P/N baseline/upstream/downstream/both model MSEs and source-sample attrition. See INDEPENDENT_2018_TEMPORAL_BQE_AUDIT.json and SOURCE_RECEIPT.json.

EEA 2018 measured annual N/P source mass is not a measured concentration delivered to each BQE station. Correlated source N/P, overlapping 2018/2022 catchments, country/EQR method differences, changing wastewater treatment status, crop and urban runoff, nonreporting and unverified official HydroRIVERS original mean **no nutrient-specific causal wastewater or species richness effect has been demonstrated**. Preserve all prior gesui and chocho results, negative as well as positive.
