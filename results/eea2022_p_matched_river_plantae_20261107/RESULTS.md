# New all-Plantae river source-positive versus source-zero pair study

Source-only selection first: https://github.com/zuizui0223/gesui/actions/runs/42377235104 .
New GBIF 2022–2024 observed source run: https://github.com/zuizui0223/gesui/actions/runs/42377454765 .

Fixed before new vegetation data: 18 nonoverlapping matched pairs in 8 geographic blocks, comprising 36 independent river monitoring locations. Source-positive sites receive modelled flow paths from EEA WWTP with **reported measured 2022 P annual load**; source-zero controls lack registered measured P source *within the 50km graph*, which does NOT prove sewage-free waters. Pairing used only flow, upstream area, cropland, population density and geography, not plant outcomes or observed in-river P concentrations.

New GBIF survey returned 1,006 distinct Plantae speciesKeys in the 36 original site buffers. The preregistered strict expected richness rarefaction to **30 records** and recording-balance gate supports only **5/18 pairs** (4 positive/1 negative), **insufficient** for the predeclared paired inferential statistic. The fixed 10-record sensitivity supports **11/18 pairs**, 7 positive / 4 negative, exact paired sign p **0.548828125** (descriptive only), mean source-minus-control log richness **−0.01699**. No robust positive source-linked plant diversity difference was observed.

These are GBIF indexed species records, not independent standardized field vegetation abundance or species-specific eutrophication indicator values. EEA actual reported source P load is a 2022 annual facility mass, not measured plant exposure, so no causal effect on vegetation can be inferred. The original chocho resource and port positives, independent 6 German TP-gradient exploratory positive, and 1,291 EU receptor negative prediction results remain distinct and unchanged.

Original raw speciesKey counts and frozen source site lists are permanently saved alongside an independent built-in-Python audit, which verifies the original source digest, all 36 sites, 1,006 speciesKeys and exact rarefaction/sign tests.
