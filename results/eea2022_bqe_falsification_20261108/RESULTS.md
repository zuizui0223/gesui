# Wastewater nutrient load and freshwater BQE 2022: reproducibility stress and falsification gates

This branch is a **follow-up to source-backed exploratory EEA benthic-invertebrate EQR prediction PR #14**, not a new confirmatory ecological effect. All original 2022 source-only site/fold definitions, original measured P/N WWTP tons and normalized EQR outcomes are unchanged. No new EEA biological outcomes were selected to favor significance.

## Significant independent data-quality finding
Original official 2026 Biology EQR table SHA256 is verified. From 3,282 source-linked river benthic records, just **2,475** have original numeric normalized 2022 EQR; **807 Polish** source river sites have missing original raw/normalized EQR and are correctly excluded. Of **394** source-positive candidates, **247 (62.69%)** are missing — original 2022 final analysis includes **147** source-positive.
For phytobenthos 3,663 candidates, **879** original normalized EQR missing (**802 Poland, 77 Austria**) and 293/403 source-positive (72.70%) dropped, leaving 110 exposed. For fish 57 Austria records missing, including 43 source-positive. Thus original year/region inference has serious reporting selection bias.
Original old model source QA had incorrectly shown **zero** invalid numeric EQR, due to pandas nullable Boolean NA propagation. This was an **accounting bug**: original model DID exclude these records; numerical fitted EQR results remain unchanged and the QA counts are corrected here. The missing site scores cannot be safely imputed.
[Actual original source QC](https://github.com/zuizui0223/gesui/actions/runs/42438230754).

## Agricultural alternative load is not adequately sampled
EEA [Waterbase Emissions 2026](https://www.eea.europa.eu/en/datahub/datahubitem-view/5bd950b1-52b9-42f3-b0d7-bf51ff3056b7), first public data source reviewed, has 13,185 records for 2022 and source-coded 2022 regional NP1 **agricultural diffuse** N/P emissions. Correct source categories and no hierarchical double count; kg/a or t/a normalized to tonnes/a, with original measured/estimated/modelled method retained. The data are reported for RBD/subunits, NOT exact river segments.
Only **142** source-linked benthic candidate sites from **Lithuania/Netherlands** have regional 2022 agriculture P emissions (all **modelled**, not measured), including 20 upstream-WWTP-positive; phytobenthos **184** sites (Lithuania only), 20 source-positive. Therefore the predeclared cross-country P/N–EQR effect cannot be robustly adjusted for independently reported agricultural N/P with this public source. Unreported country/catchment loads remain **missing**, NOT zero.
[Original source crosswalk](https://github.com/zuizui0223/gesui/actions/runs/42439118473).

## NEW exploratory source-amount permutation falsifier
The original benthic EQR N/P load model gains about P+1.10% and N+1.40% relative heldout MSE. A **posthoc** permutation of **annual load amounts ONLY among original upstream-WWTP-source-positive stations**, keeping all source-zero sites, countries, original 5 spatial folds and (in strict version) river-Q quantile strata fixed, was run 199 times each nutrient. Actual original annual-load station assignment had smaller MSE than **all 199** permutations for **both P and N** in the benthic group, within-country and within-country×Q strata: Monte Carlo extremeness (1+0)/(199+1)=**0.005**, exploratory only.
For phytobenthos the *strict within-country×Q* permuted-load null beat or tied original **42/199 for P** (Monte Carlo 0.215) and **2/199 for N** (0.015), although the actual geographic heldout MSE gain is only +0.16–0.18% and loses cross-country/geo robustness. Do not promote it to another taxon-wide mechanism.
This permutation is **not a randomized experiment or a confirmatory p-value**. Source load exchangeability within country/Q is unverified; agricultural runoff, urbanization, river engineering, source nonreporting, and P/N mass collinearity remain. It supports the narrower hypothesis that the reported **magnitude** of wastewater discharge load has some extra predictive arrangement conditional on registered facility presence among observed sites, NOT wastewater-caused EQR change.

## Next required independent corroboration
Require country-independent 2022 agriculture/source loading coverage, fix official WFD significant-pressure reporting codes without using outcome-derived pressure labels as exogenous confounders, independently validate the river-source graph against original HydroRIVERS, and test an **unopened** future region/year cohort with original EQR methods and source-complete years. The existing 2022 physical exposure+prediction cannot establish direct N-vs-P cause.

All raw original Biology/Emissions archives are with EEA; this branch publishes only matched source-only derived counts, sample QC, original source hashes, a station–regional-source lookup and quantitative exploratory permutation results, without rehosting raw big EEA zip files.
