## Publisher source census and semantics

Exactly 542 unique sequential taxon IDs; identical native taxon name/family in State table and Traits table and exact 542 numeric reference-sheet taxon IDs. Three non-taxon reference rows (separators/generic references) excluded from row joining. All raw positive adult state codes agree with source transformed ADF codes, zero discrepancies. Source 9th Hc hostplant-coincidence state present but not explained in reviewed eight-category definition; retained as metadata, not ecological endpoint. Primary An = animal feeding, predominantly dung, potentially carcasses; Mi = mineral/wet substrate use including mud puddling. Source has 41 An=1,380 An=0,121 NA and 102 Mi=1,320 Mi=0,120 NA. Raw coded zero is no documented coded use, not a confirmed behavioral absence. Missing stays unknown; processed ADF positive tokens cannot distinguish raw zero from missing in partially coded taxa. This is species-level reported resource-use states, not physiological Na/protein preference, resource quantity, actual carrion events or local adult observations. For example, original Papilionidae Mi has zero positives in this database, despite other literature documenting swallowtail puddling; treat cross-source behavioral coverage as a major constraint, not a biological assertion. Validate positive and negative coded states separately against observation/experiment sources before making family rankings.

## Biological direction and next gate

The strongest prior butterfly results were plant N-affinity/consumer host N-affinity correspondence, species-specific host identity association within Lycaenidae genera (two catalogues), and persistence of host correspondence across time. Prior observed geographic wastewater-associated within-Lycaenidae HNS prediction (PR95 +14.62% after controls; country-heldout uncertain) must not be mistaken for a causal N effect. Initial German/Austrian 2022 Lycaenidae photo-share gain not replicated by Danish standardized counts; prior-state/denominator/individual-species dependence retained (PR24,26,37,40,43). Adult feeding opportunity may contribute to local adult recording/aggregation, while larval host resources and ant dependence may constrain establishment; no such mechanism identified yet. This contrast motivates the new data axis and source-specific hypotheses, not a rescue of failed source attribution.

Next freeze same-support 454-panel analysis before fitting: use the exact common species support across adult traits and larval host affinity/availability; document observed trait variation and coverage by region, family and genus. Predeclared primary adult non-floral resource joint state (neither/Mi-only/An-only/both) on adequate support, with An and Mi as nonexclusive indicator summaries; for rare or collinear strata do not report independent substrate-specific coefficients. A joint any-An-or-Mi indicator may test broad documented non-floral use, never a single nutrient physiological axis. Family conditioning and within-genus comparison only if support; separate source-level static W, measured chemistry and habitat/population/recording controls. Compare against larval host-only and host+prior state models on fixed support, preserving original H6 and time folds. Do not assume W is adult food availability. Direct substrate behavior needs validated photographs or published observations/experiments with independent observation opportunities; standardized Danish counts offer separate population-index check, not proof of same-place aggregation.
