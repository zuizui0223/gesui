# 2022 measured river phosphorus × EEA wastewater sources × Lycaenidae: independent source-coverage bridge

**2026-11-09. Source-only feasibility, before any new river TP concentration outcome analysis.** This research protocol is informed by the posthoc 2021–2023 butterfly photo-reporting audit, thus **NOT** independent preregistration of the previously observed associations. Existing `gesui` original 2022 WWTP-source versus Peters TP chemistry PR #9, `gesui` Lycaenidae photographic reports PRs #20–#27, and `chocho` 22/25 introduced-host potential-resource expansion remain unchanged.

## Why this is a different empirical question

The DE/AT 2022 WWTP reported phosphorus-source geography predicts an exploratory fraction of public 2022 Lycaenidae *photo observation records*. After previous butterfly photo composition is included, the source incremental 2023 photo prediction **worsens (−7.41% MSE gain)**, so no direct wastewater benefit is established. Total WWTP reported N/P *mass* alone does not demonstrate that phosphorus was present in downstream environmental water or even the substrate used by adult puddling butterflies. Before claiming a mechanism, assess **actual measured river total phosphorus (TP) station coverage** on exactly the same 2022 source/photo geography. Waterborne TP is not organic protein or sodium and riverine TP concentration is not itself butterfly behavior.

## Stage B — independent publicly reported WISE SoE **Waterbase Water Quality ICM 2026** support audit

- Official EEA Waterbase ICM 2026 dataset was published July **7, 2026**, time series through 2025, with WISE-6 water data separately as `DisaggregatedData` (original observed sample dates/values) and `AggregatedData` (reported annual site-year summaries). **Do not assume DisaggregatedData and AggregatedData have the same station rows**; the official source says they are complementary reporting modes.
- Official **Discodata SQL over HTTPS** `https://discodata.eea.europa.eu/sql?query=...` query *only source catalog/schema and count/grouped station ID/coordinates/2022 date eligibility* for original `parameterWaterBodyCategory='RW'`, `observedPropertyDeterminandLabel='Total phosphorus'` (actual label to be source-verified), `countryCode in ('DE','AT')` and reference 2022. Probe documented `[WISE_SOE].[latest].[Waterbase_T_WISE6_DisaggregatedData]` and `[WISE_SOE].[latest].[Waterbase_T_WISE6_AggregatedData]` with **TOP 1 only** first; never assume a table exists or an SQL failure means absent water quality stations. Lock original response URLs, status, JSON response SHA256, and whether the official `latest` view is updated before doing expensive country/year filters. Check if `v2r1` vs `latest` aliases differ; **do not guess/fabricate 2022 TP record counts**.
- Because Discodata WISE-6 tables can lag the official 2026 zip according to EEA, an unsuccessful or stale Discodata source probe must trigger a **source-access limitation**, not a claim of missing national 2022 water-quality data. The official 2026 source downloadable CSV/SQLite is valid potential alternative, subject to file size/licence.
- Source dictionary `DisaggregatedData` reports site ID plus scheme, analyte code, sampling date, original concentration value and LOQ quality flags. Any future numeric TP model must explicitly harmonize units/TP-vs-phosphate/LOQ and original site-country IDs, **not** confuse TP, phosphate, and reported annual point source P load.

## Stage C conditional — what would genuinely count as mechanistic progress?

Only if a source-only metadata merge and repeated 2022 sample coverage passes the original 42-cell thresholds, assemble an outcome model using original **observed in-river TP concentration** at exact monitoring stations, with their original 2022 sampling date, river flow/dilution and confirmed direction-connected WWTP source as the **environmental** outcome/mediator. Then independently test photographic Lycaenidae reporting share versus a measured river-TP covariate with stable source supports, simultaneously controlling WorldCover built/crop/land fraction, 2021 plant-photo effort, geographic country blocks and 2021 butterfly-photo share. Even positive results would **not** demonstrate P causal attraction to adult Lycaenidae, puddling protein availability, or larval host plant nutrient suitability. A direct chemical mechanism requires accessible water substrate organic N/Na and independent behavioral photo annotations.
