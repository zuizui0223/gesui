## Interpretation
Existing plant nutrient affinity/host affinity and species-specific host spatial correspondence remain separate results, with their earlier limitations. This test does not show adult resource-use traits reorganize along W conditional on available larval hosts. A meaningful distinct next axis is actual adult resource opportunity (wet substrate, salt/mineral availability, organic feeding substrate and documented intake, measured separately), rather than fitting more W transformations. No direct carrion, sodium/protein, exposure, abundance or ecological trap inference. The original 746 iNaturalist annotation audit has observer/selection dependence and no confirmed feeding; no behavior imputed from labels.
