# Canopy × municipal effluent P: source-first identification protocol (2026-11-22)

Status: exploratory, posthoc design. NO new exposure coefficients, ecological fits or causal claims.

## Frozen candidate cohort
EPA NRSA 2023–24: prior independent original UID/SITE_ID/date join found 111 visits/88 sites with same-visit benthos, riparian canopy and an upstream *registered* POTW outfall with calendar-year positive TP DMR reporting. Exclude 1 visit with unknown benthic method: 110 visits, 87 sites, 78 HUC8, 33 states. Wadeable 23 visits/18 sites; boatable 87 visits/69 sites. These are candidate availability counts, not exposure or representative national samples. Preserve original IDs privately; do not post original restricted identifiers.

## Source gate before any benthic outcome
1. Reconstruct exact native NRSA UID, SITE_ID, DATE_COL, HUC8, sample method, XCDENBK and where present XCDENMID, original counted benthos; verify zero mismatched site/date, no duplicated UID, missingness.
2. Recover frozen original upstream permit+external-outfall feature pair mapping from signed NLDI/ECHO source artifact. Validate original 50 km upstream distinct-reach direction; keep downstream placebo and same-reach unknown separate. Do not treat unreported as zero or current 2026 register as proof of historical operation.
3. Re-read official EPA ICIS DMR FY2023–FY2025 for original parameter 00665 (TP) with exact permit+feature, EXO, effluent monitoring location, non-NODI numeric, original monitoring period end, native units and statistical base. Record 30/90/365-day *prior* windows from original NRSA collection date; same-day and future observations separate. Do not equate fiscal year and calendar year.
4. Only if unit, statistic and time basis are comparable, calculate an outlet-level prior-90-day TP concentration summary (mg/L), with number of observed periods and censor flags. If valid flow and compatible time basis are available, derive source-specific mass-rate sensitivity; never infer mass from concentration alone. Multiple upstream outlets require defensible aggregation, not unweighted concentration summation. Preserve zero versus missing.
5. Independently count distinct sites, HUC8, states, outfalls and variation in exposure and canopy, by wadeable/boatable method. Quantify overlap of low/high canopy and low/high measured exposure; compare monitoring intensity and geography. Missing exposure is NOT unexposed.
6. Source GO only if at least 40 distinct NRSA sites, 25 independent HUC8, 5 states, 20 genuine reporting outfalls, and ≥10 sites in each of four exposure×canopy median cells WITH comparable prior-window TP; require >1 nontrivial exposure value per HUC8 group and nondegenerate design. These are new exploratory thresholds fixed before new outcomes, not a preregistered study. Otherwise SOURCE STOP; do not loosen thresholds after inspecting signs.

## If GO: explicitly exploratory biological model
Primary response is an original source-defined benthic pollution-sensitivity measure, separately defined for wadeable and boatable surveys; never pool incompatible raw metrics. Fit Y ~ standardized log1p(exposure) * standardized canopy + bed embeddedness + stream width + urban/agriculture + season + eco-region and method where applicable. Treat HUC8 as clustering unit, report uncertainty and spatial block holdout. Primary interaction predicts positive coefficient only if higher Y means higher sensitivity. Separate alternative interaction with ambient stream TP to distinguish a sewage-specific claim from generic nutrient response. Quantify interaction prediction gain beyond main effects. Downstream-outfall placebo, alternative 30/365 day windows, reporting-density adjustment, and leave-region-out diagnostics are required. No posthoc selection of most favorable window or response.

## Interpretation
An observed interaction would be a landscape-conditioned association, not proof that riparian restoration mitigates sewage harm. No observed interaction does not prove no mitigation. Existing NRSA 2018–19 Chla×canopy interactions were null, and physical substrate effects offer competing explanations. English ChemPop RHS canopy/shading original survey-level source remains a separate, unverified validation route.
