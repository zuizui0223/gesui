**2026-11-09, new posthoc temporal-quality study** nested on the 2022 source-frozen German 8×8 matched-quartet river TP study (Draft PR #32). The original **7 complete quartets**, source-minus-registered-zero mean +0.09045 log TP, interval [−0.1816,+0.3633], and leave-one-quartet fragility are known **before** this study was designed. Do NOT present this follow-up as preregistered independent confirmation or reselect quartets to make a positive result. The goal is to test whether source and reference rivers were actually sampled close enough in time to support a same-calendar-date or same-month comparison.

## Stage 1: source-only original 2022 date coverage (NO numeric TP concentrations)

Stream original 36GB CSV from source-pinned original ZIP; read ONLY 11 metadata fields through sample ID, never `resultObservedValue`, LOQ numeric, or any TP concentration. For the *exact original 32 source-frozen station hashes*, collect unique full observed `YYYY-MM-DD` dates and distinct YYYY-MM months. Check original per-station raw 2022 TP sample row count agrees with earlier fixed 32-site QC (no hidden station substitutions). No re-selection of all 115 same-river pairs or national monitoring sites.

For each original eight quartets:
- Source arm 2-station day intersection and registered-source-zero control arm 2-station day intersection; repeat at month level.
- **Strictly matched-four-sites SAME DAY**: intersection of all four exact calendar dates. Require **≥3 common exact 2022 sampling days** in at least **4 source-only original quartets across ≥3 original country×2° blocks** to permit a quantitative four-station *same-day* TP-gradient contrast.
- **Same-month control fallback (separately fixed secondary)**: intersection of all four calendar months. Require **≥3 common 2022 calendar months** in at least **5 original quartets across ≥4 original country×2° blocks** to permit a four-station *same-calendar-month* TP-gradient comparison. This does NOT mean upstream and downstream were sampled on the same date or that all monthly values observe the same effluent pulse.
- If either support gate fails, STOP THAT OUTCOME without relaxing date/month thresholds. Record original eight quartets, original study's seven annual-median results, and failures. If both fail, NO new numerical river TP model.
- Original source-only output includes all dates/months per hashed site and quartet exposure labels, source file/QA SHA256, source dates counts, per quartet exact 2-site and 4-site support and explicit independent geographic-block counts. **Commit this full source-only output before opening any new numeric daily/monthly source TP values.**

## Stage 2 (only for support-passing tiers): original water TP numeric date harmonization

After Stage1 committed and confirmed:
- Original individual TP `resultObservedValue` must be finite/nonnegative in `mg{P}/L`. Drop below-LOQ true/1 flags without substitution; unexpected/non-numeric flags excluded. Datewise site medians used to prevent treating same-day assays as independent replicated stations, then **within-month site median of valid day medians**.
- Re-evaluate ≥3 common qualified SAME DAY dates (if strict gate passed) or ≥3 common qualified MONTHS (if secondary gate passed) after LOQ and valid-result filters; require same originally fixed number of qualifying quartets and geographic blocks or STOP. No replacement quartets.
- Four-station contrast on each eligible exact date/month: source-pair `log(TP_down+0.001 mg{P}/L)−log(TP_up+0.001)` minus source-zero-control-pair same log gradient. The +0.001 constant is retained from frozen original PR32; no seasonal or threshold tuning.
- Aggregate mean of these four-arm matched time-level contrasts within original quartet, then *equal-quartet* mean. Do not weight quartets by number of days/months; report quartets separately, sign count, source-interposed reported annual original P mass range, and exact 2^n quartet sign-flip **descriptive** reference plus 9,999 quartet-level bootstrap 95% descriptive interval, seed 20261109. No causal randomization p-value.
- Sensitivity: fixed leave-one-quartet-out mean range. If a single original quartet reverses sign, report instability. Compare original annual-median 7-quartet +0.09045 for context but do not call the same-day or monthly comparison a confirmation of annual source response when different samples/periods.
- STOP before any Lycaenidae response analysis. Even a reproducible same-day WWTP interposed TP gradient would only concern measured water TP, not adult protein/sodium puddling, larval fitness, ant partners or local Lycaenidae abundance. WWTP annual reported P source t/year and monitoring TP mg{P}/L are not time-matched doses.
