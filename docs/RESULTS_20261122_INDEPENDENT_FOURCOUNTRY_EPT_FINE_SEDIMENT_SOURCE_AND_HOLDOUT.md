## Previously frozen four whole-country target holdouts: observed results

[Country holdout model protocol](https://github.com/zuizui0223/gesui/blob/analysis/external-fourcountry-finesediment-ept-source-20261122/docs/PROTOCOL_20261122_EXTERNAL_4COUNTRY_COUNTRY_HOLDOUT_EPT_DESCRIPTIVE.md) was saved **before** country sediment–EPT correlations or model effects. Exactly four holdouts, each training the model on the original other THREE countries only. Baseline = three-country equally weighted grand mean of relative EPT, one vote per country. Full = weighted original mean and **fixed linear Ridge α=10** on original fine sediment fraction (0–1), slope penalized, intercept unpenalized. The original relative EPT fraction is the exact source authority-verified name E/P/T relative abundance sum; all other taxa incl. UNKNOWN stay in the publisher denominator. EPT upper bound = known EPT + whatever uncertain `Insecta` mass remains, not silently zeroed. No tuning on target country, no new outcomes, nonlinear search or mixing USA EPA.

## How it relates to the US EPA source-domain problem

- The four-country data support **weak descriptive country-heldout transport of a sediment-cover relative-EPT predictor**, broadly consistent with prior *already published* relationships of fine sediment to community composition. It cannot test the EPA's main source-status interaction, because it has no EPA historical HUC8/source frame or shared categorical `old source represented` variable.
- Original published visual fine cover <2mm ≠ USA field `XEMBED`; original EPT published normalized family/order proportions ≠ raw US EPA `TOTAL300` counted sample. These different measurement definitions limit cross-system calibration.
- Thus **new actual reproducible results** here are source integrity, taxonomy harmonization and fixed descriptive country-heldout MSE gains. They are **not** proof of a novel ecological mechanism or sewage-driven aquatic/terrestrial transfer.
- The sewage-source EPA POTW TP×canopy available-site failure remains [gesui PR #139](https://github.com/zuizui0223/gesui/pull/139) SOURCE STOP, untouched.
