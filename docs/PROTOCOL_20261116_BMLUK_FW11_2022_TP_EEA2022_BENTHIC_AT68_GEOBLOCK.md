## Pre-frozen descriptive vs external comparisons (posthoc as a new dataset discovery, not confirmatory trial)

- Physically same government PK (FW...) and international PKINT (ATFW...) water station; source year=2022 unfiltered total-P annual mean, vs EEA normalised_EQR original 2022 benthic condition. All true-source-site 68 retained, no post-hoc exclusions by result or original WWTP reporter/nonreporter.
- Primary environmental predictor = ln(original annual TOTAL unfiltered phosphorus-P mg/L). Express adjusted coefficient per one source-cohort predictor SD. No intercept-only ecological claims; all P values must finite >0 and EQR finite and physically plausible.
- Full-sample raw Spearman rank correlation and Pearson logTP–EQR as descriptive only.
- Fixed **five source geographic region_3deg_block groups** to avoid sharing one geographical block between held-out and fitted data. Baseline independent aquatic model = intercept + log1p(real source river discharge DIS_AV_CMS) + log1p(original catchment UPLAND_SKM) + scaled latitude + scaled longitude. Expanded adds the original logTP. Train-only scaling, ridge penalty alpha10 all predictors except intercept, equal evaluation weight to each of the five source geo blocks, compare 5-block-disjoint out-of-block MSE gain. Also report each held-out block's baseline/additive MSE and direction, not just pooled positive.
- Full-sample conditional OLS original EQR regression adjusted for the same source background covariates, with block-level leave-one-out coefficient stability. The 5 original region blocks are too few for robust conventional block-cluster inference and original EQR is a national index with method/year uncertainty; **no nominal confirmation p-value or causal mechanism inference from blocks**.
- Source measurement uncertainty: mean river TP over whole 2022 and benthic EQR source year 2022 are not necessarily same-day. Original annual mean includes a treatment of below-quantification concentrations as half the reported LOQ in publication, distinct from raw 3-day EEA strict sampling gate; do NOT infer original sample dates from means.
- Crucial wastewater specificity STOP: the original 225 GZÜV sites near 117 2022 report-positive wastewater outlets yielded just **3** same-year annual TP sources in this yearbook, only 2 with 2022 EEA benthic identities, one with 955m government vs EEA coordinate mismatch. Exact HydroRIVERS graph source #43874361627 verified BOTH benthic sites are UPSTREAM of original source outlet reaches; **0 actual directly downstream co-located wastewater TP+benthos cases**. Hence a positive Austria TP–EQR association is **not** wastewater-specific, and cannot be pooled with France73 TP–EQR as causal sewage or water→land propagation.
- No observed insect emergence, adult puddling, local foliar nitrogen, actual plant biomass, host utilization, wastewater-specific nutrient contribution, or ecosystem services is extracted in this independent aquatic source test.
