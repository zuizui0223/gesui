## Initial source-chain findings to challenge

- Real annual-report upstream P + source river Q/area/geography adds heldout logTP predictive MSE improvement +5.292%, four of five blocks positive. Same raw downstream placebo +1.065%, four of five blocks positive. Original river TP→EQR +33.896% 5/5 blocks positive. Raw upstream P→EQR adds +0.467%, whereas downstream placebo raw P→EQR adds +13.295%. When original measured river TP is already conditioned, upstream raw annual source P has **−2.096%** incremental heldout predictive gain, while downstream placebo raw P adds **+14.379%**. Hence the *sewage-source-specific direct EQR mechanism* is not established.
- Converting upstream reported 2022 annual mass tonnes/year to potential unattenuated source concentration by original mean river Q yields **+16.302%** heldout TP prediction and **+19.269%** EQR prediction; source chemical concentration conversion factor exactly 1e9 mg per tonne / (365*24*3600*1e3 litres per m³) = 0.03170979 mg P/L per t/year per m³/s. This potential concentration is NOT the actual delivered concentration nor an instrument measurement.
- An unfair asymmetrical negative control (upstream P/Q versus downstream P without Q) would exaggerate directional specificity. Therefore **test the exact SAME Q normalization for the downstream original placebo** on the same 68 sites and folds, plus both normalized terms jointly.
