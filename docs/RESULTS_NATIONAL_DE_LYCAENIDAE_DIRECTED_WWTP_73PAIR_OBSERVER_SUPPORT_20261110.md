## Interpretation and stopping decision

The original 2022 1° WWTP P/photographed Lycaenidae relative-share positive and its 2021–2023 historical/landcover falsifiers remain accurate **spatial reporting composition** results. Neither original WISE6 water-chemistry monitoring points nor iNat opportunistic plant photographs are standardized butterfly habitat transects. Even an upstream/downstream observation could reflect natural downstream habitat changes, tributary nutrient inputs, agriculture, population density, host plants, and photographer behavior.

This attempt adds a **larger independent source/observation-process frame**, but confirms no local, source-specific Lycaenidae photo-effect inference possible under the declared effort and independence rules. It does **not** show zero actual wastewater effects, nor show that all Lycaenidae respond to nitrogen, P, sodium, proteins or host subsidies. No adult mud-puddling or larval host response was measured.

### Next evidence-bearing direction

Stop re-fitting the same weakly supported iNaturalist local circles. A new study, not a rescue sensitivity, should start from repeated **standardized butterfly transect/15-minute event surveys** in regions with enough source-interposed and sourcezero river sites **and documented survey effort**, ideally with source-to-downstream connectivity, real nutrient/substrate chemistry, host vegetation and ant partners. eBMS provides a standardized monitoring framework but **public site-level/source-year coverage near these rivers is not established**; this is a new source feasibility task, not a claim that usable observations are immediately available.

Stay Draft/unmerged; no modifications to original chocho manuscript, its 22/25 Lycaenidae potential host-resource range positives, or gesui's independent 2018/2022 benthic EQR evidence.
