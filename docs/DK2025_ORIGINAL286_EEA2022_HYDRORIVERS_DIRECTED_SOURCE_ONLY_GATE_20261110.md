**Strictly retrospective/posthoc after earlier 2025 Lycaenidae 3-/5-family, calendar-month and WorldCover outcomes.** This new analysis is physically meaningful **SOURCE/GEOMETRY ONLY** and must NOT access or select on butterfly outcomes. Source direction is a route of potential *water transport*, not proof that butterflies or host plants accessed wastewater. No new 2025 butterfly response is authorized unless the original fixed source-geometry support threshold passes. No changes to earlier positive/negative published aggregate findings.

## Physical QC: fixed BEFORE newly modeled direction groups

1. Read the WHOLE source-verified Europe graph with fields `HYRIV_ID`, `NEXT_DOWN`, `LENGTH_KM` and WGS84 line geometry. Clip to an expanded Denmark-plus-border region bounding box **6°–17°E and 53°–59°N** after checking source schema. Do not infer downstream from latitude, river proximity or 2021 WorldCover cover classes.
2. Snap the exact 286 original eBMS site *point centroids* and 203 registered WWTP freshwater discharge points independently to the nearest HydroRIVERS line at **<=500 m planar distance in EPSG:3035**, with deterministic nearest-distance, smallest-HYRIV-ID tie-break. Record failed snaps separately rather than guessing a reach or replacing the point. Some 500m source-site proximity is not actual riverbank sampling.
3. For each site and every registered outfall at <=5 km straight-line Haversine, use only verified directed reach edges `NEXT_DOWN`. Explicit mutually exclusive classifications: **DOWNSTREAM_CONNECTED <=10 HydroRIVERS route km** if site reach is strictly downstream of source reach, **UPSTREAM_CONNECTED <=10 km** as spatial negative direction, **SAME_REACH_ORDER_UNKNOWN** when both lie on same reach, **CONNECTED_BUT_ROUTE_GT10KM**, **BOTH_SNAPPED_BUT_NO_VERIFIED_DIRECTED_ROUTE <=50KM**, **SITE_REACH_NOT_SNAPPED**, **SOURCE_REACH_NOT_SNAPPED**. Treat all disconnected/unknown as **NOT VERIFIED**, not unexposed. Across additional predeclared 10/25/50 network-km thresholds report route sensitivity but do not change near/remote site selection.
4. If multiple original WWTPs lie within 5 km of an eBMS site, retain **all** per-site source candidates and give a separately aggregated source count to avoid calling one nearest source uniquely causal. Only count distinct original registered outlet SHA and independent 5 km geodesic-connected clusters; station point rows are not independent treatment sites. Exclude same-reach points from evidence of downstream transport because within-reach order is unknown.
5. **Source-only ecology feasibility threshold**: at least **12 distinct eBMS site centroids from >=8 distinct original WWTP outfalls**, representing >=5 separate 5-km outlet components across >=3 original 1° regions, meet **strictly directed downstream <=10 network-km**, each site <=500m from HydroRIVERS, with original >=2 published-Occurrence-linked source dates. Also require >=8 upstream connected sites from >=4 source groups across >=3 original 1° regions as potential negative-direction controls. Without these, **STOP before any downstream/upstream Lycaenidae analysis**. This gate is an opportunity threshold, not a power calculation or guarantee of comparable habitat.
6. Even with a passed gate, require standardized butterfly transects to be actually adjacent to receiving-water / riparian habitat and receiving-water nutrient or organic/Na chemistry to identify biologically meaningful exposure. A centroid within 500m of a stream cannot confirm butterfly adult puddling or larvae using riverbank plants. Remote>=10km from qualifying EEA outfalls is not source-free: other registered or unregistered sources can contribute via rivers.
