## Original independently selected source hydrology (unchanged)

The source file is an already-frozen 2022 Germany WISE6 water-chemistry comparison: eight original strict WWTP-interposed versus registered-qualifying-source-zero same-directed-river four-station quartets, **32 distinct original national 2022 WISE6 TP monitoring stations** and public original geocoordinates. They were chosen on source-reported 2022 EEA N/P outfalls, directed reaches and matching geometry **without** butterfly photo observations (before this new study).

An upstream monitoring point, a downstream point, and an original WWTP outlet strictly between their corresponding river reaches provide **water-station proximity** hypotheses; the actual downstream butterfly sampling habitat is unknown. An original WISE6 sourcezero comparator means **no qualifying registered intervening WWTP**, not no wastewater/no pollution. HydroRIVERS converted third-party topology is still not independently verified byte-for-byte against the official v1 original.

**Both independent pre-outcome effort gates FAIL.** Outcome API calls were deliberately NOT attempted, and no 2022 Lycaenidae positive/negative local response, fitted logit, p-value, adult protein/salt puddling, larval fitness or population trend can be inferred.

### New observation-process asymmetry diagnostic, NOT ecological effect

The same **2019–21** nonbutterfly plant-photo counts vary by **0–616** (2km) and **3–2,038** (5km) despite identical site windows and public filters. Unmatched raw photo-report opportunities can easily create a false family-share gradient if one selects river areas solely by WWTP source status.

Across all eight original source-selected quartets (including ones that fail the study's inference gates), posthoc descriptive 2019–21 photo effort log(1+counts) contrasts:

- Source-registered river pair mean minus registered-source-zero river pair mean: **+0.705** (2km; 6/8 positive, descriptive exact 8-quartet signflip fraction 0.3125), **+0.293** (5km; 5/8 positive, fraction 0.6641). No statistically robust uniform source-versus-zero observation effort imbalance at the quartet level.
- Four-arm downstream-minus-upstream difference-in-differences of these nonbutterfly photos: **−1.546** (2km; four positive/four negative, reference fraction 0.2031) and **−1.275** (5km; two positive/six negative, fraction 0.1172). Their sign and magnitude should **not** be read as true downstream plant ecology, or any butterfly response.
- A single original 5km sourcezero downstream site had **2,038** plant photos while its sourcezero upstream counterpart had **40**, highlighting nonuniform public documentation. Public plant photos are a mixture of habitat, richness, and observer effort, and may themselves be influenced by historical sewage geography: this is a **quality check rather than a guaranteed treatment-negative biological control**.

## Outcome and next valid scientific branch

**Conclusion:** The all-8 direct WISE6 original sample can physically distinguish registered WWTP-interposed water station reaches, but public photographic observer support is too heterogeneous to support the matched river Lycaenidae study at nonoverlapping 2km or 5km radii. The original 2022 P-source × butterfly family photo-share positive remains **coarse geographic photographic reporting**, not a demonstrated downstream biological response. This is a **successful availability audit**, not a biological null.

Do NOT respond to failure by loosening 4-arm photo effort gates, enlarging overlapping circles, comparing butterfly photographs from one successful quartet, or replacing the original quartets based on favorable butterfly outcomes. A new independent effort-matched wide source frame could be declared before outcomes, but it must be a genuinely separate prospective study; standardized repeated butterfly transects near physically connected river reaches would be more defensible. In particular, direct puddling resource use needs adult behavior and biochemical substrate N forms/Na/protein, rather than WWTP reported annual P/N.
