# German/Austrian open standardized butterfly event metadata discovery — source-only gate

2026-11-08. This is a **new source-finding step**, nested under the published Norway/Flanders source-only results. All original `chocho` larval host resource expansion (22/25 Lycaenidae), `gesui` 2018/2022 EQR positive predictive results, and the failed 2022 butterfly near-WWTP observational-support gates remain **unaltered**.

## Fixed decision before discovery

**Question:** is there a lawful, *publisher-original, openly downloadable* source of **2022** Germany/Austria standardized butterfly survey **Event records** with site-level positions suitable for prospectively checking their proximity to verified EEA 2022 measured positive both N/P direct-freshwater discharge points *without reading species/count responses*?

1. Official GBIF dataset registry `/v1/dataset/search` is metadata only. Predefine 12 textual query strings: `Tagfalter-Monitoring Deutschland`, `Tagfaltermonitoring`, `Viel-Falter`, `Butterfly Monitoring Austria`, `Schmetterlingsmonitoring`, `Lepidoptera monitoring Germany`, `Lepidoptera monitoring Austria`, `BMS Germany`, `BMS Austria`, `Austrian butterflies`, `German butterflies monitoring`, `butterfly transect`. Freeze candidate raw dataset metadata (publisher, licence, dataset type, DOI/UUID, publication update dates, *source publisher* archive endpoints), no taxon occurrence query.
2. Do not accept `OCCURRENCE` or `CHECKLIST` results as standardized visit denominators, and do not imply a metadata-only catalogue is downloadable Event data. A verified `SAMPLING_EVENT` **original** DwC-A archive with Event core is required to advance.
3. Once a candidate publisher Event core is available, read **only** `meta.xml` and `event.txt`; source year must include **2022**, original eventDate day-resolution, an independent transect/site key and ≥2 repeated survey visit dates. Published coordinate uncertainty ≤500m; missing uncertainty is not zero. Do not open occurrence extensions or species counts. Licensed originals only; respect data-use restrictions. Predeclare exact site/source join and matched region before outcome access.
4. Source matching uses original EEA 2022 annual reported measured N/P, unique full reporter+plant ID and exactly ONE direct FW discharge point, not a nearby WWTP building. Source-only distance **≤2km**, prespecified sensitivity **≤5km** and remote **≥10km**, including site footprint and coordinate uncertainties; require ≥10 independent near sites and ≥10 remote sites across ≥2 shared regions/counties **before any butterfly species outcomes**. Fail transparently if conditions not met; do not alter thresholds after inspecting coverage.
5. Regional scope: official Germany TMD is fixed volunteer transects since 2005, datasets **on request** ([source](https://www.monitoringzentrum.de/monitoringprogramme-biodiversitaet/tagfalter-monitoring-deutschland-tmd)); the Austrian Viel-Falter national 400-site programme began in **2023**, while Tirol started in **2018** and Vorarlberg in **2020** ([source](https://www.abol.ac.at/en/project/viel-falter-monitoring-2/)). **Do not assume** nationwide Austrian 2022 data exist.
6. If no open Event-core 2022 dataset is found, record a verified discovery limitation and prepare a narrow privacy-safe **aggregate survey-site coverage request** to eBMS / German UFZ / Austrian Viel-Falter **without sending anything**. Coordinator can internally join sensitive transect locations with supplied EEA outfall source IDs and return near/far site counts by region, no precise protected coordinates. Official eBMS scientific access approval is coordinator-managed ([access](https://butterfly-monitoring.net/ebms-data%20access)).

## Why this remains biologically important

The unresolved mechanism is a potential *discordance between adult resources (water/protein/sodium puddling) and larval host quality/resource geography*. Neither original `chocho` potential host distribution change, generic butterfly photo count, nor a reported WWTP total N/P mass measures a Lycaenidae adult feeding behavior, larval survival, ant interaction, or actual substrate chemistry. Positive numbers at separate grains are retained, not conflated.

This source-discovery project is Draft, and cannot by itself establish an ecological effect or negative biological association.
