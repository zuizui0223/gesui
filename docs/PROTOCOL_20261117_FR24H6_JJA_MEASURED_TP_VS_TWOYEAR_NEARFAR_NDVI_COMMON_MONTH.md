## Important limits
Positive TP↔dR would suggest *same-season same-place environmental association* at observational scale, not wastewater-specific nutrient subsidy or transfer. Negative or zero does not prove absence of a physical cross-system edge. TP is not exact municipal treated-effluent N/P dose; near/far images use center-point circles, not riverline perpendicular distances; NDVI is greenness not standing mass, carbon, floral nectar, insect abundance, or pollination services.
