## Locked directed source and negative control

- Convert original 187 public station positions and all original eligible direct-FW WWTP outlets from WGS84 to EPSG3035 and find nearest original pinned river reach, STRICT maximum 500m station and outfall snap. If two original reach IDs tie within 2m of the minimum, mark ambiguous and exclude; do not choose the river giving a favorable TP concentration.
- Follow original directed NEXT_DOWN reach link from each source. Accumulate positive N/P annual source loads and count ONLY when monitoring reach lies on a strictly DOWNSTREAM different reach, within maximum **50km** along original LENGTH_KM. Same-reach source/receptor excluded because within-reach order unknown. For every receptor, also count WWTP origins on strictly downstream reaches within 50km as a directional negative-control placebo. Disconnected geographic neighbors must remain absent.
- Label a station registered-source-positive if >=1 original eligible qualifying WWTP source is strictly upstream by directed graph; controls are **no QUALIFYING REGISTERED 2022 WWTP source**, not definitively untreated water. Record missing reach, tie, country, downstream placebo, original annual P/N mass, source counts, original HydroRIVERS modeled mean flow DIS_AV_CMS, modeled catchment UPLAND_SKM, source and receptor snap distances, all original source/API hashes. Do not claim HydroRIVERS mean discharge represents actual 2022 flow.
- Compute an apparent source-load / modeled flow index only for positive mean flow using total upstream reported P t/yr × 1e9 mg/t divided by modeled average flow m3/s ×365.25×86400s ×1000 L/m3. This is a *dilution proxy*, NOT measured TP concentration or physical source fate.
- **Before numeric river TP re-open for directed model**, among 184 independently numeric-quality sites, require >=20 strictly source-positive stations AND >=20 source-zero stations, >=5 source-positive stations in each DE and AT, >=5 independent country×2degree positive geographic blocks, >=15 distinct positive exposed HYRIV_ID reaches. Source-only failure STOPS the river TP regression; zero sources is not an environmental TP null.
- If source-only support passes, compare 2022 measured station log1p(TP mg P/L) with identical country/latitude/longitude, training-only log modeled DIS_AV_CMS, log UPLAND_SKM and 2021 WorldCover country-cell built/crop/land-area covariates, then add strictly upstream 2022 registered WWTP P mass. Use leave-one-country×2degree regional block out (no outcome-driven tuning), downstream P mass negative control, country-level results, and missingness. The unit is **individual monitoring stations/reaches**, not a single 1degree butterfly-photo cell.
- Independent aquatic station status/quality flags were not fully checked in original numerical TP extraction beyond LOQ/date/unit: this is another required pre-publication gate.

## Interpretation and previous results

This can test **water source→river TP** physical plausibility only; it does not measure adult puddling protein/sodium, larval fitness, ant partners or actual Lycaenidae abundance. Original 2021–2023 public butterfly *photo-reporting share*, 2022 1degree source geographical associations, failed chemical bridge, and original chocho 22/25 introduced-host geographic potential expansion must all stay separate. If source connectivity is insufficient, report failure and do not weaken thresholds to rescue significance. Third-party river topology caveat applies to any source-connected result.

Do NOT commit original large raw source archives to the repository; only exact source QA and site/reach source-only exposure metadata with hashes.
