- **Germany 630** unique verified public FW outfall sources (`DE_UWWT_2022`), **Austria 526** (`AT_UWWTD_2022`). **1,156** pooled unique public sources, no butterfly site/count data in either source.
- Permanent public source manifest: [CSV](https://github.com/zuizui0223/gesui/blob/analysis/lycaenidae-de-at-public-event-discovery-20261108/results/lycaenidae_de_at_2022_source_only/EEA2022_DE_AT_PUBLIC_BOTH_NP_MEASURED_ONE_FW_OUTFALLS.csv). Source QC and original REST receipt: [JSON](https://github.com/zuizui0223/gesui/blob/analysis/lycaenidae-de-at-public-event-discovery-20261108/results/lycaenidae_de_at_2022_source_only/EEA2022_DE_AT_PUBLIC_WWTP_NP_SOURCE_QC.json). Original public-source CSV SHA256: `926ab310904be048d5d36bbfebd5eabfe0b318279800142f2e2a86bee924f4ce`.
- Do not call these **measured environmental protein/salt/soil N dose**. Annual reported effluent load is not direct water/substrate chemistry at a butterfly. Individual lab measurement date is not independently established. These are geographic sources for feasibility assessment only.
- Scientific source-side target: number of independently revisited butterfly transects in **2022** geometrically proximate to verified 2022 EEA public original discharge points, not counts of Lycaenidae individuals. Original location protection should remain with monitoring coordinator.
- Follow source-only preoutcome decision rules inherited from PR #22: independent site/transect with at least **2 distinct 2022 field-visit dates**, original location/route uncertainty **≤500 m**, choose transect whole-route within **≤2 km** nearest source for primary or **≤5 km** secondary; **≥10 km** fully remote as comparison. Conservative route-footprint + position uncertainty bounds. Nearby 2–5 km ≠ proven wastewater exposure.
- Feasibility gate for downstream ecological work: at least **10 independent 2022 near** and **10 independent 2022 remote** transects in **2 or more within-country common regions** (e.g., Bundesländer or Austrian Länder); common standardized protocol and visit denominators. Report all counts, including non-eligibility, without changing the bands if support fails. Prefer sites/counties where both groups coexist.
- This is a **request for aggregate feasibility screening only**. At this stage do NOT request original fine coordinates, observers, sensitive transect routes, species/individual butterfly counts or any suppression-risk small cells. If feasible, a subsequent approved research/data-use agreement can discuss deidentified per-visit data.
- Public GBIF dataset registry source-only search [metadata audit](https://github.com/zuizui0223/gesui/blob/analysis/lycaenidae-de-at-public-event-discovery-20261108/results/lycaenidae_de_at_2022_source_only/GBIF_PUBLIC_DE_AT_BUTTERFLY_EVENT_DATASET_REGISTRY_QC.json) checked 12 predefined terms / 101 unique metadata records, but no matching 2022 German/Austrian published butterfly **Event-core** archive confirmed. The single DE SAMPLING_EVENT candidate is arthropod-trap metabarcoding, **not** standardized butterfly transect monitoring. This is a finite search, not proof that no dataset exists.
- Existing publication-facing result remains separate: `chocho` potential host geography expands for **22/25** Lycaenidae in the established host data, but this is **not** a causal sewer/WWTP effect. `gesui` 2018 and 2022 river benthic EQR prediction successes are a separate biota/response.

Would it be possible for your data management team to perform a **feasibility-only internal spatial screen**, using TMD's protected exact transect locations without sharing them with me? I would be grateful for aggregate counts of **independent 2022 transect sites with at least two separate dated survey visits**, reporting, within each federal state or other suitable region:

1. The number of qualifying transects whose entire route is within **2 km** of one of those publicly listed 2022 freshwater outfalls;
2. The analogous count within **5 km**, as a prespecified secondary screen;

Our prospective source feasibility criterion is at least **10 independent near and 10 remote transects across two regions with both groups**. If those criteria are not met, the ecological analysis will stop without examining the butterfly outcomes. Even if they are met, a source-proximity association would not by itself establish wastewater chemical exposure or a causal effect.
