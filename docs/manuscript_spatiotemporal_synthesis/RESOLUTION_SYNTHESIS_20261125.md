# Resolution-aware evidence synthesis — 25 November 2026
## Manuscript decision
This addendum supersedes the interpretive emphasis, not the archived numerical results, of MANUSCRIPT_PLAN_AND_ABSTRACT.md. The main claim remains the contrast between spatial correspondence and limited incremental temporal predictability on the same French catchment frame and the European butterfly forecast. The additional England analysis provides a separate taxonomic-resolution sensitivity, not another link in a sewage-to-butterfly chain. Neither environmental filtering nor resource-mediated selection has been identified causally. Retain all nulls, including catalogue nonreplication and population sensitivity. Do not keep screening these opened data for a positive mechanism.

## Revised working title
**Spatial correspondence and resolution-dependent predictability of river and terrestrial assemblages in wastewater-associated landscapes**

## Revised English abstract (working draft)
Environmental gradients and resource associations can structure assemblages across space, but spatial predictability need not extend to temporal change or establish trait-specific filtering. We examined these distinctions using European catchment and river-monitoring data, separating reported wastewater discharge, measured receiving-water chemistry and modelled wastewater geography. At 263 repeatedly sampled French river stations in 73 catchments, total phosphorus improved geographically held-out prediction of benthic ecological quality in 2018 and 2022 (+30.8% and +22.7% mean squared error reduction), but not prediction of four-year change (−0.4%). In the same catchment frame, plant nutrient-habitat affinity improved prediction of butterfly host-habitat affinity conditional on phosphorus (+30.2%), whereas phosphorus did not improve terrestrial-index predictions. Across 133 European catchments, host-resource information provided small improvements in forecasts of later Lycaenidae recording composition for 46 species using two host catalogues (+0.33% and +0.26%). In the four-window 2014–2025 comparison, host-resource change and concordance did not improve forecasts of butterfly change on common quality support. Host-specific spatial correspondence persisted across nonoverlapping periods and within-parent-catchment comparisons, but was attenuated after conditioning on earlier butterfly composition. In an additional English river dataset (591 sites), three measured nutrient-associated chemistry variables improved prediction of the between-order component of benthic family tolerance scores (+11.84%), not the within-order component. The modelled effluent fraction improved the within-order score component after chemistry (+1.72%, seven of seven regions), yet its association was not extreme under an order-preserving score-label null (p=0.355). Thus, prediction at a particular spatial, temporal or taxonomic resolution is not by itself evidence of a corresponding ecological selection mechanism. Catalogue dependence, observational sampling and uncertain source attribution preclude a demonstrated wastewater-mediated aquatic–terrestrial pathway.

## Results interpretation and stopping rules
1. England: three chemistry summaries add +10.559% [5.062,14.290] total BMWP and +11.841% [6.936,15.982] between-order prediction after EDF; within-order −0.411% [−1.163,0.385], joint score-label null p=.936. EDF after chemistry adds −0.519% [−0.761,−0.253] total, −0.568% [−1.265,0.055] between, +1.718% [1.283,2.259] within (7/7 regions). The conditional EDF score-specificity p=.3548 fails. Neither predictor demonstrates within-order tolerance-score filtering. EDF is a static modelled effluent fraction, not measured delivered dose. The three chemistry summaries are matched to biological sampling dates, not necessarily instantaneous same-day samples, and are not exhaustive water chemistry.
2. Terrestrial: regional pool conditioned host identity predicts spatial correspondence beyond family × early incidence expectation in both catalogues; this is a comparison of associations, not a held-out model or permutation significance test. Congeneric residual raw r=.169696 and .094530, conditional r=.088936 and .037065. Prior-consumer-adjusted joint criterion fails, and ordinary parent resampling includes zero in both quality estimates. Do not claim a dynamic host effect, genus-only consumer response or phylogenetic mechanism.
3. Cross-domain comparison: plant EIV sorting and host trait HNS analyses use different taxonomic levels and response definitions from the English score decomposition and actual host graphs. No direct hypothesis test comparing fine-resolution effect strength across compartments; no claim of a general hierarchy of environmental versus interaction filtering. Predictability can motivate candidate mechanisms; stronger source attribution, independent standardized observations and a priori treatment/dose information are required to distinguish them.
