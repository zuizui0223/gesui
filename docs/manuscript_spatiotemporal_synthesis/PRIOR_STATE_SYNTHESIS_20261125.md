# Prior-site state and environmental predictability — synthesis addendum

This updates the interpretation of the earlier resolution synthesis, without replacing any source results. Main anchor remains the matched French river/catchment comparison and the fixed butterfly forecasts; the TREAM and English repeated-site analyses are additional tests on different observation units, not independent replications of a common causal effect.

## Central result
Distinguish persistent assemblage structure, transferable predictors of state, and incremental predictors after prior local state. In TREAM, fine family composition persists within monitoring projects across disjoint halves (ten insect orders r-like reproducibility .5745; EPT .6594). Macrobasin differences account for 26–28% of cross-half spatial covariance in restricted support, but raw basin centroids do not improve leave-site-out transfer. Historically weighted own-site pooling improves the later three-year transition by 23.00% and 33.71%; after climate and land-cover correction, +23.07% and +33.94%. Those environmental increments themselves are uncertain. This is conditional normalized family incidence, not total biodiversity or abundance.

In England, 131 repeatedly observed sites in six regions support historical-only, held-out-region BMWP score prediction. Earlier own-site score adds +51.18% [32.75,60.31] relative MSE reduction after background, three measured nutrient summaries and static modeled effluent fraction; within-order score component +41.98% [5.76,57.00]. Before own-state adjustment, chemistry after EDF adds +12.45% [7.21,18.22] to total-score prediction. With own state, the increment is +0.57% [-0.03,1.17]. In a post-result paired contrast using the same prior-free MSE denominator, the difference is 12.20 percentage points [7.09,17.73]. The pre-fixed within-order EDF increment after chemistry and own state is +0.638% [-0.131,1.801], fails the positive lower-bound criterion. Stable abundance-recording-method subset (83 sites/four regions) fails source support and was not fitted. Earlier EDF spatial score-specificity null remains p=.3548. Different response definitions preclude quantitative ranking or pooled effect sizes.

In European butterflies, existing actual-host resource correspondence survives geography-conditioned nulls and nonoverlapping observation periods; conditioning on prior consumer composition weakens the independent association, and host change does not robustly forecast consumer change. These analyses do not establish a wastewater-to-terrestrial causal pathway.

## Claim and boundary
Working title may retain spatial correspondence and resolution-dependent predictability. The strongest common insight is that prior local state carries information not captured by selected environmental covariates, while apparent spatial associations and predictive improvements do not alone identify dynamic effects or causal ecological mechanisms. Prior states can reflect persistent habitats, historical exposure, observation process, or biological feedback. Conditioning on prior state can remove a true legacy effect, so lack of incremental forecast gain is not evidence of absence of nutrient or sewage impacts. England has six regions, not six countries; TREAM excludes UK/France but site overlap with other prior surveys not exhaustively ruled out. These are retrospective tests on opened outcomes, not prospective external replication. Do not conflate state persistence with community memory, hydrological connectivity, or material transfer.
