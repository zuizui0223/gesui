## Why a request is scientifically necessary

The EU 454 H7×period GBIF HNS data are **aggregated**; they cannot yield unique true near-river and inland species observations. Our existing source-only site audit of open Swedish SeBMS in 2022 found 264 point centers (127 with >=5 seasonal survey days). Authentic 2022 EEA SE369 eligible registered single-FW outfalls total 14; of 127 repeat-5 centers, only six nearby relations to four eligible source outfalls were physically screened and no strict 1–5km directed reach pair was confirmed under full-reach approximate routing. Prior DK 2025 eBMS had only 2 directed downstream outfalls; German original 2022 WISE6 photo-effort controls failed. None of these show source-specific butterfly biological effects or that effects are zero.

The single primary posthoc-but-frozen 2022 design requires >=24 independent same-H7 source/reference reach pairs across >=12 authentic H6 basins and >=3 countries; 8 locations per pair (source vs registered-source-negative reference, downstream vs upstream, near 0–200m vs inland 500–1500m), each with >=5 observer dates, >=20 five-family records, >=2 numeric-HNS Lycaenidae, and >=70% HNS completeness. No distance, year, primary taxa, support thresholds or analysis may be changed to rescue failures.
