## Fixed numerical endpoint, models and falsifications

**PRIMARY unit:** one original HydroRIVERS receptor reach, collapsing the two repeated-reach pairs into **168 independently identified reach units**. For a reach with multiple qualified sites, 2022 actual TP = *median of their original WISE 2022 site-year TP medians*; original source P/N annual mass and count must be exactly identical for the same reach. For consistent cell land cover/country, take arithmetic mean of source-derived station lat/lon and original 2021 WorldCover built/crop/land area; group by same original country×2° block. Additionally report original **170 station-level** analysis as preregistered sensitivity. Do not remove sites based on TP magnitude. If site sources disagree within reach, stop rather than choosing one.

**Primary 2022 measured water endpoint**: log1p(original qualified original TP mg{P}/L annual site median), site-year TP as daily median then site-year median from original source, no belowLOQ substitution. Original numeric site-year medians have been frozen at `results/lycaenidae_2022_measured_river_P_bridge/2026_WISE6_2022_MEASURED_TP_SITE_YEAR_MEDIANS_AND_42_LYCAENIDAE_CELLS_QC.json`. This is water chemistry, not butterfly abundance or puddling.

**Fixed baseline:** country indicator, latitude, longitude, log1p(original HydroRIVERS modeled `DIS_AV_CMS`), log1p(original modeled `UPLAND_SKM`), independent original ESA WorldCover2021 country-cell built and cropland fractions and log1p(country terrestrial land area km²). Train-only standardize numeric predictors; if a training fold has singular design or zero country-specific source variance, report failure, don't tune or drop a feature based on response.

**Sources tested separately**, each added to the identical baseline: within-country train-only standardized log1p(**strictly upstream original 2022 registered P tonnes/year**), original reported N tonnes/year, original verified WWTP count, **strictly downstream P tonnes/year** directional placebo, and separately a potential original reported P load divided by modeled river discharge mg/L equivalent (NOT actual chemistry or dose). P reported mass is the original source-P geographic primary predictor, not a known station load. Do not enter WWTP P/N together as independent causal sources.

**Fixed cross-region test:** leave one complete country×original 2° geographic block out. Compute unweighted reach-level source-added vs baseline mean-squared error (MSE), % improvement 100×(MSEbase-MSEadded)/MSEbase, MAE; site-level sensitivity and DE/AT separately, plus exact country-source-positive and registered-source-zero raw TP medians reported as descriptive **not causal** differences. Use original 2° geographic block prediction-error bootstrap (999 draws, seed 20261109), not a causal CI. Compare upstream source P result to strictly downstream P placebo *on identical reach units and baseline*.

**Additional fixed negative controls:** 199 permutations of upstream source P **within country** and 199 permutations **within original country×2° blocks** (seed 20261109 + fixed deterministic offsets). Each permutation refits identical geographic heldout procedure; empirical random-label gains≥observed fraction with plus-one correction. Neither randomization represents randomized treatment; within-region source labels can be strongly autocorrelated.

**Source specificity cautions:** Site-level annual median may reflect industry/agriculture/geology/legacy upstream sources; positive registered source not actual P dose, absence of qualifying source not pollutant-free, hydrologic graph is third-party GeoParquet conversion not independently topology-checked against official HydroRIVERS. Original samples are limited to WWTP-source-positive 1° geographic cell/cohort previously selected for Lycaenidae photos; never generalize estimates to all DE/AT rivers without reweighting an independently defined national station population.

**STOP protocol:** source-only 170 QC panel must pass >=20 positive/20 registered-source-zero, >=5 source-positive per country, >=15 positive original river reaches and >=5 positive 2° blocks before numeric source model. No negative/zero result is discarded. If primarily upstream P and modeled load/flow show positive out-of-region prediction while downstream-only placebo does not, this is environmental source-to-receptor **observational evidence**. It is not direct confirmation that treated effluent caused TP or that Lycaenidae nutrition changed. Source-only 2021–2023 photos and 22/25 original `chocho` resource potential positives remain separate.

Additional unreviewed EEA `resultObservationStatus`/expert QC flags and official HydroRIVERS topology comparison remain publication limitations, regardless of predictive sign.
