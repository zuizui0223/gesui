## Fixed post-source-only ecological analyses (all explicitly exploratory)

1. **Habitat spatial confounding**: compare near vs remote distributions of built, cropland, grass, tree, wetland and surface-water fraction; summarize 1° region distributions, 500m spatial unit only. Check whether original near/remote habitat overlaps in the same 1° region (positivity). Test balance without treating each survey date as a new landcover unit.
2. **Original 2025 butterfly endpoints**: read only already-archived SHA-checked licensed per-site aggregate Lycaenidae/Pieridae/Nymphalidae/Hesperiidae/Papilionidae counts and dates; no new butterfly occurrence downloads or family reclassification. Replicate the original near/remote 3-family and 5-family proportions and Lycaenidae count per documented date before any habitat adjustment.
3. **Strict area/habitat strata**: within each already fixed original 1° region, classify WorldCover grass+tree into pre-fixed low (<25%), intermediate (25–75%), high (>75%) groups, and built fraction <10% versus >=10%. Retain only strata with >=2 near and >=2 remote sites AND >=5 published-Occurrence-linked dates per side; report support counts and original source IDs. Use matched-period count-rate per day and 3-family/5-family shares, weight strata by min(near recorded days, remote recorded days). Never tune cut points post outcome.
4. **Full continuous sensitivity**: original 1°-region fixed-effects OLS on each site-year Lycaenidae log(1+records) minus log(published days), plus region, fraction built, cropland, grass, tree (omit one compositional land-cover class to avoid exact collinearity) and log original linked survey days; and separate logit count share among 3 or 5 families (only target family positive sites, no invented 0/0). Use low-N geographic region bootstrap as descriptive, not confirmatory inference. The goal is whether direction is explained by habitat, NOT to find a significant positive effect.

## Mechanistic boundary

WorldCover grass/tree/crop class is **not identified larval food-plant abundance**, ant mutualist habitat, nitrate/phosphate bioavailability, or adult puddling sediment nutrition. No source-to-receptor river transport or in-stream substrate is measured. A reduction of near/remote source differences after adjusting land cover could reflect environmental confounding or selection induced by habitat/observer distribution, not proven mediation or absence of sewage action. If habitat cannot be measured/source positivity fails, archive a clean failure instead of inferring cause.

Source raw COG and licensed eBMS archive must NOT be committed. Commit site hashes and original WorldCover class counts/fractions with source tile URLs and QA only, keep original `gesui` PR #40 Draft and leave unrelated `chocho` manuscript unchanged.
