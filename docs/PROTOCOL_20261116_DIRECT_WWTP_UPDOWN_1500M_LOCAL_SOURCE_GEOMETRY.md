## Research decision: why not use the old 16-outfall × 48 reach-centroid GBIF pilot as a local treatment test?

Original source authentic [GBIF plant scoreability + geometry Actions #43845610859](https://github.com/zuizui0223/gesui/actions/runs/43845610859) reuses exactly the 16 independently preselected HydroWASTE sites and 48 U2/U1/D1 river reach centres from prior PR #3/#4, plus the independently authentic original 4,011 scored EIV species. EIV source availability is adequate for **13 of 16 outfalls** (all three centres have >=10 numeric-EIV species, >=40% species-key coverage and >=20 recorded occurrences of scored species), across **four countries**. But the original upstream U1-to-downstream D1 segment-center river-path separation is **4.096–16.479 km (median 8.076 km)**: NO site triplets within a <=2 km local treatment comparison and only 2 within 5 km. Original HydroWASTE pilot has NO actual source-reported annual N mass in those 16 facility records; modeled water fraction is NOT a delivered nitrogen or phosphorus load. These are source feasibility, not a test of wastewater impacts.

Therefore inventing "outfall-proximal riparian vegetation effects" from those original 48 mid-reach centres would be invalid. The following source-only design supersedes their physical layout, NOT the original GBIF records, results or validation.
