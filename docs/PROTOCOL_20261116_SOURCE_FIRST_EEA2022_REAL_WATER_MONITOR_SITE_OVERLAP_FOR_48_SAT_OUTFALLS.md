## Why aquatic data availability before satellite effect fitting?

A single wastewater aquatic–terrestrial paper needs **same actual source outlets, field receptor water quality and aquatic biota, and connected riparian land**, not France 2018/22 river TP/I2M2 sites concatenated with an Austrian/German satellite pilot. The DE/AT official EEA benthic BQE source in current main contains only 68 actual AT 2022 sites and **0 DE 2022 sites** in the original source-eligible EEA2022 2,475-site frame; original EEA2018 has one AT and no DE site. This is a source-coverage property, NOT no German aquatic organisms or no biological wastewater effect.
