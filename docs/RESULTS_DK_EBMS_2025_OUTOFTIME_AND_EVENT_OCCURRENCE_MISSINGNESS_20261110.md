# Denmark 2025 out-of-time Lycaenidae standardized counts, rebound and reporting-completeness audit

**2026-11-10. New year 2025 outcome was opened ONLY AFTER independently committed 107-site 2025 Event-only support gate SUCCESS. This is a descriptive temporal validation of a previously known German photo-positive / Denmark count-negative pattern, NOT a wastewater treatment or biological causal test.**

The prespecified **simple negative source-proximity direction** from 2023–24 is again present in 2025, on BOTH the observational count-per-original-Event-date and pooled family-share endpoints. No Papilionidae individualCount in the focal Denmark panel, so the three-family share is effectively Lycaenidae relative to Pieridae. Annual source near share is a population- and observer-weighted source-location summary, not an effect per independent WWTP.

**Critical temporal counter-result:** 2024→2025 Lycaenidae count per original Event date **RECOVERED**: near **0.155 → 0.480** (3.090×), remote **0.801 → 1.014** (1.266×), near/remote temporal rate-ratio ratio **+2.441×**. Therefore the 2023→2024 near-registered-source decline is **NOT a monotonic ongoing decline**. Do not call a permanent WWTP-driven fall.

- Same-site same-2024/2025 calendar-months: near versus remote relative 2025/2024 Lycaenidae rate-ratio **2.294×**, with May–August version **2.095×**.
- Same-site calendar months shared in all three 2023–2025 years: 2025/2024 near versus remote ratio **3.734×**; May–August **3.788×**. This is an explicit after-the-fact sensitivity, with count and survey dates fixed by original Events.
- 2024→2025 family controls for all 107 sites, original Event-day rates: Pieridae near/remote temporal rate-ratio 1.505; Nymphalidae 1.540; Hesperiidae 0.772. The Lycaenidae rebound is not the only family temporal change near WWTPs. No causal effect inferred.

## 3. NEW serious audit issue: Event with NO Occurrence extension is not proven zero butterflies

A separate original full DwC-A **Event primary-key ⇄ Occurrence core-ID** audit (no species identities or counts opened in this stage) showed different publication-completeness between location classes and years.

| Public original survey documentation | 2023 near | 2024 near | 2025 near | 2025 remote |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Original Event rows | 119 | 244 | **215** | **706** |
| Events with any linked Occurrence row | 66 | 122 | **113** | **301** |
| Sites with NO Occurrence rows in entire site-year | 7/23 | 2/23 | **8/23** | **6/38** |
| Site-years with ≥2 distinct dates having any Occurrence | 10/23 | 15/23 | **14/23** | **28/38** |

**One ENTIRE original WWTP-near outfall group (7 repeated site rows and 28 original 2025 Events) has ZERO published Occurrence rows in 2025.** Therefore those site-years are not independently established zero-butterfly detections; they may reflect all-zero events OR incomplete Event/Occurrence publication. This also limits the original full 2023/24 annual report. The 2025 source system with real documented 2025 occurrence rows is only **3 of the original 4**, so 2025 direction is not four independently observed WWTP cases. The apparent absence of Nymphalidae and all other monitored families in those seven sites reinforces caution but does not diagnose the publishing mechanism.

An Event count is NOT automatically an observed complete butterfly census. The initial 2025 all-Event-day denominator may be too large in source-near areas if unlinked rows represent unreported surveys. Conversely requiring any Occurrence can select recorded-positive events and bias towards detectable butterflies. Both versions must be reported together; neither is causal.

## 4. Independent linked-Occurrence date sensitivity — same sites, same years

Posthoc quality sensitivity requires at least **two distinct calendar dates with ANY original Occurrence row in both 2024 AND 2025** at the same original site, independently of which butterfly family was present. This leaves **14 near and 27 remote** original sites, versus 23/38 when using all original Event dates. All 2023–25 common-site source years with at least two linked dates gives **10 near and 26 remote** sites.

| Exactly same 2024/25 sites; using dates with any published Occurrence | Near 2024 | Near 2025 | Remote 2024 | Remote 2025 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Source-qualified same sites | 14 | 14 | 27 | 27 |
| Dates with ≥1 published Occurrence | **112** | **112** | **300** | **292** |
| Lycaenidae individualCount | **34** | **95** | **494** | **585** |
| Lycaenidae per linked date | **0.304** | **0.848** | **1.647** | **2.003** |
| Lycaenidae share among target families | **19.1%** | **39.1%** | **51.3%** | **64.6%** |

The near-source 2025 Lycaenidae recording deficit still persists on these linked-Occurrence supported sites (**0.848 vs 2.003 per linked date**, 39.1% vs 64.6% family share). **2024→2025** near/remote family recording rate-ratio ratio is **2.297×** (near 2.794; remote 1.217), independently matching the recovery direction seen with all original Event dates. In the all-three-year linked-date subset, near 2023→2024→2025 Lycaenidae rate was 1.276→0.330→0.979 per linked date, remote 1.559→1.675→2.049, further showing nonmonotonic 2024 trough.

**These are selected observational recording-completeness sensitivities, not a preregistered effect or correction for true detection probability.** The 2025 source group with seven entirely unlinked sites is **excluded from the 14 documented near sites**. No randomized WWTP treatment, within-reach nutrient dose, host substrate, landscape-phenology matching or independent source-level inference is possible.

## 5. What this does and does not establish

**Supported:** 2025 repeats a pooled near-source deficit in recorded Lycaenidae counts/shares on exactly the same original standardized eBMS site panel; the 2024 near-source downturn reversed substantially by 2025, and the direction remains after same-calendar-month matching and a posthoc any-Occurrence-linked-date sensitivity. Pseudoreplication and uneven published-event coverage limit every result. **No WWTP-induced negative or positive effect is identified.**

**Not supported:** a sustained sewage-driven decline, source P/nutrient-mediated adult puddlers, wastewater chemistry at a butterfly habitat, gene/host/ant mechanism, or a generalizable national causal effect from only three to four near-outfall groups. Presence of 2022 registered WWTP source at ≤5km is not proof of hydrologically downstream exposure. Disappearance of original Occurrence rows in one near group is a reporting ambiguity, not confirmed insect extinction. A broader independent monitoring panel with at least 10 true outfall systems and matched hydrological receiving habitats would be required for mechanistic WWTP inference.
