## C. FIXED regional-block predictive statistics (conditional only on full B passing)

1. **First relationship (environment):** Cell-level original `log1p(median of observed 2022 river TP mg{P}/L)` as response. Compare original geographic **country, lat, lon, independent ESA WorldCover2021 built-up fraction, crop fraction and log1p(country terrestrial land km²)** baseline, versus same baseline + within-country train-only standardized `log1p(official 2022 WWTP reported P effluent tonnes/year)`. Both are **cell-level** summaries: cell reported outfalls need not be upstream/connected to the individual TP measuring station; this comparison is **co-distribution**, not delivered dose. Add separately N mass and reported outfall count as secondary source axes, no joint P/N mechanism due collinearity. Original **leave-one-country×2° block out** OLS (train-only centering and scaling), unweighted 1° cells, zero tuning. Report MSE/MAE, per-country predictions, and source increment % regardless of sign. A P effect is not causal.
2. **Second relationship (butterfly photo reporting):** Original exact same eligible 1° cells, response `log((2022 Lycaenidae photos+0.5)/(2022 Pieridae+Papilionidae photos+0.5))`, which measures **photo-recording share, NOT actual butterfly abundance**. Baseline country, lat, lon, 2021 WorldCover built/crop/country land, original 2021 *Plantae* photo reporting opportunity, and original **2021 Lycaenidae photo share logit and 2021 3-family photo effort**; then add source-reconciled cell median actual 2022 river TP. Use same country×2° heldout OLS, train-only standardization and exact same site coverage; report incremental MSE and coefficient, and compare with 2022 source mass P predictor separately.
3. **Third nested comparison** with fixed same-cell baseline+reported EEA source P first, then cell median observed TP separately, to evaluate whether river TP contains any independent predictive information beyond infrastructure + historical butterfly recording geography. Do not fit or claim statistical mediation, no evidence original WWTP source to station hydrologic connectivity.
4. A required temporal specificity negative-control: same river TP source from 2022 must not be described as an experimentally new exposure in 2022 or 2023. Conditional same-sample 2023 public family photo share comparison may be shown, clearly labeled posthoc, if year metadata all pass.
5. **Falsification:** original environmental TP vs P mass relation can be positive, zero or negative; 2022 butterfly photo association with TP can be positive, zero or negative; report each separately. If the WWTP source does not predict actual 2022 river TP after land-use and geography, or measured TP does not add photographic prediction beyond history, the proposed P-mediated ecological chain is **not supported**. Even if both predictive, causal wastewater→butterfly nutrition and adult puddling remain untested.
6. **Geographic test units**: 1° cells repeated 2022 observed TP sites within the country are not independent organisms or equivalent water reaches; holdout uses original 2° groups, but 21 spatial groups do not imply complete causal exchangeability. Land-use/drainage/observer/host resources and site selection remain confounded.
7. No threshold changes after observing TP numerical values; no repeated source selection, no imputation for measured TP; no low-LOQ site replacement.
