## Genuine official source and ambiguity

Austrian government BMLUK public WISA dataset site https://www.bmluk.gv.at/themen/wasser/wisa/daten/daten.html and H2O https://wasser.umweltbundesamt.at/h2odb/ explicitly offer multi-domain queries for physicochemical water sampling, macrozoobenthos/phytobenthos samples, and EmReg-OW load summaries attached to water monitoring stations. The public WISA JS frontend https://wasser.umweltbundesamt.at/wisa-datenabfrage/ does not by itself demonstrate an actual machine-downloadable 2022 observation table, or that all three outcomes are sampled at the same river station in 2022.

The public Austrian catalog https://www.data.gv.at and H2O website also refer to national GZÜV flowing-water station GEODATA. Public EU data.europa.eu offers a documented catalogue search API https://data.europa.eu/api/hub/search/ . Use this official catalogue metadata and genuinely public direct official distribution links; never assume an old CKAN API, invent a private WISA REST endpoint or bypass required login. EmReg-OW source load aggregated to monitoring stations is not itself a single known EEA annual reported outfall mass or measured nutrient delivery.
