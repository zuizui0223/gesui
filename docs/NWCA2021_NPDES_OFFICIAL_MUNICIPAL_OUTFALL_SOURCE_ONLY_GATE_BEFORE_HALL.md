- Original 2021 EPA NWCA national probability wetlands: exact first-visit probability site population originally frozen as 920 sites, with original **568 matched vegetation+water chemistry 2021 sites** (SITE_ID+VISIT_NO), original file SHA in `EPA_NWCA2021_ORIGINAL_VEG_WATERCHEM_SITEONLY_GATE.json`. Earlier 499-site quality subset was examined prior to this wastewater project, therefore final effect analyses are not prospectively independent. Original 48-state 499-site negative TN–Shannon result remains separately reported.
- NEW official EPA ECHO ICIS-NPDES Discharge Points dataset `https://echo.epa.gov/files/echodownloads/npdes_outfalls_layer.zip`, inside `NPDES_OUTFALLS_LAYER.csv`, ~50 MB compressed, continuously updated **weekly**, source SHA and `2026-11-10` retrieval UTC recorded. Facilities and *permitted feature/outfall* coordinates are provided; **if the outfall-specific coordinate is unavailable EPA substitutes the FACILITY coordinate**, which is not a physically verified discharge location. Previous NOAA coastal layer has warned that reported outfall points may not be actual water discharge points. Use primary group with `LATLONG_TYPE` explicitly indicating feature/outfall ONLY, sensitivity all coordinates with facility-coordinate proxy separate.
- Dataset includes permit ID `EXTERNAL_PERMIT_NMBR`, `PERM_FEATURE_NMBR`, `SIC_CODES`, `NAICS_CODES`, `FACILITY_NAME`, `LATITUDE83`, `LONGITUDE83`, permit status and effective/expiration/termination dates. Municipal wastewater facility typing MUST use specific **NAICS 221320 (Sewage Treatment Facilities) OR SIC 4952 (Sewerage Systems)**; municipal owner alone is insufficient and may include power plants, other discharges. Where codes do not identify sewage, the source is `UNCLASSIFIED/OTHER` and NOT treated as a municipal effluent source. No mining facility names to optimize source richness after inspecting plant responses.
- NPDES national weekly export reflects **permit information as available in 2026, not historically complete 2021 discharge**. Even original permit effective date before 2021 does not prove 2021 N/P monitoring or release; dates may have been updated after reissuance. **No contemporaneous source or nitrogen mass measurement is claimed**. Only official 2021 DMR permit/outfall/flow and river catchment linkage could strengthen the exposure interpretation.
