## Interpretation
The specified older nutrient summaries do not robustly add prediction beyond the recent score, recent chemistry and controls. Earlier biological state may contain additional information, particularly the coarse score component, but stable habitat, observation method, exposure history and demographic processes remain unresolved. No identified legacy mechanism, no demonstration of absence of delayed effects, no historical wastewater load or source-specific exposure. Actual source chemical measurement windows must be verified before claiming strictly ordered nutrient exposure. EIDC's official catalogue explicitly dates the product by the biological sample dates (https://catalogue.ceh.ac.uk/documents/23f194b0-1e21-4f14-be9c-6eabdcc1feb7); associated chemistry date ranges require supporting documentation or raw measurement records. No further outcome-based lag selection on these 131 sites. PR170/163 results and STOPs stand.
