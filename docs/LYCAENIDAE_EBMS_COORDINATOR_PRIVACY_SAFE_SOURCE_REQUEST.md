# Next viable source: eBMS/UFZ/Viel-Falter access for 2022 transect events, without disclosing sensitive locations

**Prepared 2026-11-08; no data request has been sent and no restricted butterfly records have been accessed.** This document follows fully archived public Norway/Flanders eligibility failures; it does NOT change or relax the previously frozen 2022 near/outfall thresholds after inspecting the source data.

## Rationale from completed source audits

Open national Norwegian and Flanders 2022 monitoring data were published as Event-core archives and successfully independently audited, yet **2022 EEA source matching** yielded Norway 70 independent Lucas grids × 20 sections, but **0** grids conservatively wholly within 2 km and **2** within 5 km of a qualifying original Norway `NO_UWWTD_2022` freshwater outfall; 63 ≥10 km away. Flanders public event sites have original coordinate uncertainty 7,071m, making ≤2–5km direct-outfall proximity invalid. Both fail source-coverage gates **before** butterfly species-level occurrence/count observations are opened. New data source selection is required, not a wider radius on the old data.

The 2022 EEA original official source inventory has much more qualifying reported positive **both measured N/P** direct freshwater plant coverage in continental countries including Austria/Germany/Hungary than in Norway/Belgium. Those high-coverage countries are prospective **sampling-source priorities**, not evidence of butterfly effects.

## Verified routes for lawful scientific access

- eBMS data access is administered under the [official eBMS scientific-data request policy](https://butterfly-monitoring.net/ebms-data%20access). The request includes a license form and coordinator approval; source says coordinators should approve within two weeks of request. Approval is NOT automatic.
- Germany's [Tagfalter-Monitoring Deutschland (TMD)](https://www.monitoringzentrum.de/monitoringprogramme-biodiversitaet/tagfalter-monitoring-deutschland-tmd) is standardized transect monitoring since 2005, led by UFZ/GfS, with detailed data **available upon request**, not openly downloadable as event-level coordinates and counts.
- Austria's [Viel-Falter butterfly monitoring](https://viel-falter.at/monitoring-en/) reports systematic visits of fixed Austrian monitoring sites and repeated expert/volunteer surveys; [methods paper data access](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10531-022-02534-2) points to eBMS access or corresponding author. Do not assume precise sites/visit counts are public.
- A restricted-network eBMS source must only be handled after explicit approval and governed by its terms. This project has not connected to or retrieved central restricted eBMS data.

## Proposed privacy-preserving first request (source feasibility only)

**Do not request Lycaenidae counts at the first stage.** Submit the scientific hypothesis, source/protocol and original official 2022 EEA FW discharge point locations/unique outfall IDs, and ask coordinators to perform the spatial join internally (no sensitive location coordinates shared back).

Request only aggregate metadata for each country/region, by distinct standardized transect site:
- Original **2022** survey dates/effort protocol: number of independent transects with ≥2 dates, coordinate uncertainty ≤500m or coordinator-verified site footprint.
- Count of those independent sites in prespecified **all-transect-footprint within ≤2km**, secondary ≤5km, and remote ≥10km from an original 2022 EEA both-positive *measured* N/P **unique direct FW** outfall (not a nearby WWTP building).
- Original locations/paths remain on the coordinator's server. Provide only aggregated counts by county/region, and optionally deidentified stable site IDs for exact no-replacement matching; never infer sensitive Flanders originals from obfuscated public coordinates.
- Count only EEA 2022 source inventory plants with a unique official reporter+facility key and one direct FW outfall; missing registry is not proof of no sewage.
- Require **≥10 independent near sites and ≥10 remote sites in ≥2 common independent subregions** per national cohort with repeated visits. Preserve earlier 2km/5km/10km bands unmodified.
- If site count fails, stop and report source infeasibility, no individual butterfly records requested.

## Only after a source gate passes

Seek approved deidentified visit-level data with stable hashed transect/site identifiers, event date, event duration/effort, total butterfly counts, explicit absence/zero-visit semantics, taxon species/family, Lycaenidae individual count by visit, sampling protocol and observer/life-stage quality flags. Require an independently agreed minimum count and common-region support; offset by visit effort and adjust weather/season, site/county, vegetation, host plant resources and land use before ecological association.

The scientific analysis can then test **relative recorded Lycaenidae abundance conditional on standardized butterfly monitoring effort** within a spatially source-eligible sample. Neither a positive nor negative response is direct evidence for adult protein/Na puddling, larval nutritional quality or wastewater causation. Independent substrate chemistry, ant mutualism and local host quality would still require new source evidence.
