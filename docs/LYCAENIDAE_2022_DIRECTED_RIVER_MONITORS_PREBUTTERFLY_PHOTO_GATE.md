- Original WISE6 Germany **eight** 2022 source-interposed versus registered-source-zero same-directed-river *four-arm* quartets, exactly **32** distinct and original public georeferenced WISE6 monitoring stations. These are the same formerly response-blind EEA WISE6 water-quality quartets from gesui Draft PR #32, **not selected according to any iNaturalist butterfly responses**. Full original sample retains all eight quartets even if one water TP quartet had unaligned 2022 months.
- Absolute site positions, original station SHA hashes, source-interposed 2022 EEA WWTP reported P emissions and sourcezero geometry are copied unchanged from original [national source-only 8×8 quartet file](https://github.com/zuizui0223/gesui/blob/analysis/wise6-2022-national-DEAT-river-TP-pairs-20261109/results/wise6_2022_national_DEAT_TP_sourceonly/GERMANY_2022_WISE6_TP_8SOURCE_8ZERO_SAME_RIVER_MONITOR_QUARTETS_SOURCE_ONLY_LOCK.json) and [301-site national TP WISE6 public registry](https://github.com/zuizui0223/gesui/blob/analysis/wise6-2022-national-DEAT-river-TP-pairs-20261109/results/wise6_2022_national_DEAT_TP_sourceonly/NATIONAL_DE_AT_2022_WISE6_RIVER_TP_PUBLIC_STATION_SOURCE_ONLY_QC.json).
- Original WISE6 stations represent river chemistry, not confirmed butterfly habitat; 2/5km circles are **proximity proxies** and do not follow water transport. A WWTP outlet strictly between the monitor reaches is **not** proof of butterfly exposure. A source-zero pair means no registered qualifying intervening source, not a pristine unpolluted river.
- Do not use eBird, unlicensed butterfly records, individual observers, photo pixels or protected species points in this phase.

## Separate source/effort gate

Run only **iNaturalist public aggregate Plantae taxon 47126**, place Germany 7207, public accurate georeference <=500m, research grade, photographed, noncaptive, unhidden. Exact site latitude/longitude point, fixed **2km and 5km radii**, **2019-01-01 to 2021-12-31** entirely before 2022 butterfly outcome. Store each aggregate original request URL, source JSON SHA256 and total_results, **not identities of observers, individual records or photos**. Respect documented iNat API policy about approximately 1 request/sec, retry limited 429 and keep all errors as NA rather than biological zero.

For each quartet, all four site circles must be non-overlapping at the requested radius, pass >=5 prior Plantae photos on *each* of its four arms and min:max photo effort >=0.15. At radius 2km, require **>=5** qualifying original quartets from **>=4** distinct original country×2° blocks before ANY family outcome photo API. Radius 5km is a separately **predeclared** more broadly sampled landscape sensitivity, allowing only non-overlapping circles and requiring **>=4** quartets across **>=3** blocks before any 2022 family query. The radii are not chosen based on butterfly effect size; neither success would prove a riverbank-specific exposure. Keep original all-8 failures and any overlaps visible.

## If (and only if) a radius passes the pre-outcome source-effort support gate

A separate workflow (not this source-only workflow) may read original public 2022 Apr–Oct iNaturalist **Lycaenidae 47923**, Pieridae 48508 and Papilionidae 47223 aggregate photo counts using the **same predeclared radius, original full quartet arm pairs, and identical geolocation/privacy/quality filters**, retaining photo-eligible quartets only if every arm has >=5 three-family photographs, and >=4 nonoverlapping independent quartets in >=3 geographic blocks. If that postresponse support gate fails, report the sample failure without calculating source-minus-sourcezero butterfly contrasts. If both gates pass, primary response is the four-arm difference of photo log odds, with 0.5 correction; test mean direction and 2^n exact sign-flip reference, report min LOO and source status caveats. No adjusting radius/years/outcomes after seeing results.

**New research claim restrictions:** even adequate 2022 photo reports cannot establish adult protein/salt mud-puddling, larval host performance, population growth, or causal WWTP wastewater subsidies. Standardized independent butterfly transects and measured larval host/substrate chemistry remain preferable. Earlier same-buffer local trials failed and must remain documented.

**Research intent:** distinguish a broad 1° WWTP source/photo association from possible source-to-downstream station vicinity butterfly recording opportunities before attributing a physical pathway. A source/effort gate failure is valid data availability evidence; it is not a biological null and cannot be re-tuned based on what would produce positive effects.
