# Source-locked France381 riparian canopy *change* 2018–2022: official Copernicus TCD 10m

2026-11-24 JST; **prospective-for-new-tree-cover-source but exploratory/posthoc overall**, following PR #149's null conditional EQR forecast. No outcome coefficients/p or site exclusions chosen from 2022 EQR or tree-cover response.

## Why this source
Earlier [PR #149](https://github.com/zuizui0223/gesui/pull/149) genuinely matched 2018+2022 same physical French benthic sites (381, 14 true 3° region blocks, fixed 5 geofolds) and showed that the original *static* Copernicus Riparian Zones LCLU2018 250–500m mosaic predicts 2018 and 2022 **spatial** EQR distribution, but not 2022 EQR beyond observed 2018 site EQR. The missing biological-habitat variable is **actual 2018–2022 local riparian landscape change**, not a redesigned cross-sectional significance search.

CLMS Riparian Zones status currently has published 2012/2018 classes and 2012–2018 change; its 2021/2024 status layers were under a new production contract announced 2026-08-31. **Do not invent a directly comparable RZ2018→RZ2022 class-change product.**

Official year-specific candidate: [Copernicus Tree Cover Density 2018–present, 10m](https://land.copernicus.eu/en/products/high-resolution-layer-forests-and-tree-cover/tree-cover-density-2018-present-raster-10-m-europe-yearly), DOI **10.2909/e677441e-fb94-431c-b4f9-304f10e4dfd8**. Must use native original **2018 and 2022** 10m TCD + original TCD confidence/quality companion, not classify forest probability from a single point, not use annual NDVI/land color replacement. TCD is canopy-density percent, **not actual annual biomass, physiological productivity, nitrogen uptake or a change in the total class composition**. Forest cuts/growth may affect EQR or vice versa, confounded by flood, drought, climate, urbanization and agricultural land use.

Alternative **if TCD source discovery fails, STOP; do not unfreeze primary**: CLMS Total Productivity (TPROD) 10m annual 2018/2022 DOI 10.2909/977e4bb8-407f-48ec-b4c4-403bca5a6a3b is a separate exploratory future study, not a replacement once TCD fails.

## Stage B only after source GO
For a source-eligible French site define fixed `∆TCD_urbanless_near=(mean native TCD_2022 - TCD_2018)` over stable originally mapped 2018 nonwater/woodland or mixed vegetation classes 3/4/5/7 within 0–250m; `∆TCD_urbanless_far` analogously 250–500m; primary `∆TCD_near - ∆TCD_far` (percentage-points of canopy density). Use same 10m raster grid pairwise-validated year, no valid-2018 invalid-2022 cells silently replaced. Report class-specific QA, change distribution, and station-to-station temporal variation. Sources cannot resolve canopy understory or changes in herbaceous plants.

**Fixed test** (not causal): response `∆EQR=EQR2022−EQR2018` from *exact same original method-stable 381-site panel*, baseline EQR2018 + lat/lon/log riverQ/logarea + 2018 static mapped broad landcover. Add only `∆TCD_near−∆TCD_far` and compare fold-heldout MSE with original 5 geographical folds, train-only scaling Ridge alpha10. Site equal and 14 original 3° block equal MSE; descriptive fixed-prediction 2000 block bootstrap, both signs and source samples including missingness. Secondary control same-site `∆TCD_far` alone; no arbitrary nonlinear threshold, no p-based radius changes, leave-POTW and Europe cross-country causality out.
The **2018 and 2022 canopy changes are simultaneous with biological ∆EQR**. A predictive association does NOT establish canopy change precedes EQR or that sewage P flux impacts either. LCLU2018 is not measured 2022; field EQR is not insect biomass or trophic emergence.
