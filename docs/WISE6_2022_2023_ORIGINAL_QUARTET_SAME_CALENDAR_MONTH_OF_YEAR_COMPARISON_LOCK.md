# Prospective-as-to-2023-numeric same-calendar-month cross-year contrast within original 2022 WWTP quartet stations

**Written and committed 2026-11-09 after source-only 2023 time gate, but before reviewing any 2023 TP numeric concentration or source-vs-zero 2023 effect.** The whole design is *posthoc relative to the known +0.169 original 2022 monthly positive gradient*. This is a sampling-season control, NOT experimental treatment.

- Immutable original **8 German four-arm 2022 quartets** with same directed river WWTP-interposed source vs no **qualifying registered** interposed source; 32 distinct original WISE6 site hashes, source 2022 EEA annual reported P tonnes. Same original 2022 WISE6 source **2022** sampling-date-only gate (pre-numeric) is versioned and source-verified. The new 2023 WISE6 source-date-only gate fixed the same 32 stations, reports 5/8 complete **2023** four-arm monthly quartets over four original 2° blocks; no complete same-day quartet.
- Pre-numeric 2022 & 2023 source-date intersection by **calendar month of year**, not actual same date: among original eight quartets, original #2 has **7** January-to-December months present in BOTH years, original #5 has **8**, #6 **9**, #7 **8**; four original complete quartets in original **3 country×2° blocks**. Others have zero overlapping four-arm months due missing 2022 or 2023 source calendars. Strict **source-only cross-year comparability gate**: require ≥**4 original quartet IDs**, ≥**3 distinct original 2° source blocks**, and ≥**3 exact same-calendar-month-of-year overlaps** per original quartet. Gate **PASSES**, all IDs fixed without looking at 2023 P concentrations.
- Only **after** original 2023 numeric TP same-month sample support gate and original 2023 numeric TP source quality have passed, compute the original same-four-arm gradient for each **2022** and **2023** observation at identical quartet and calendar month-of-year, using verified original 2022 and new 2023 country-matched independent WISE6 water samples. Formula log(TP_source_down+0.001)-log(TP_source_up+0.001) minus log(TP_registeredzero_down+0.001)-log(TP_registeredzero_up+0.001). Average **month-of-year paired differences within quartet first**, then average across original four independent quartets with equal weight. Evaluate 2022→2023 year difference (new year minus old) and raw 2023/2022 same-month expected signatures, exact 2^4 sign flips of **quartet-level year differences**, and a descriptive 9999 quartet resample CI. **Only 4 original quartets = small, cannot establish absence or causation**. If fewer than four match after LOQ/status/quantified date QC, STOP before across-year difference summary; record missingness.
- This 2022/2023 comparison is a **time-stability falsifier**, not a pollution intervention: the 2022 WWTP reported annual P sources could be operating in both 2022 and 2023; their true monthly 2023 P loads, actual flow and dilution, and time-varying land use/tributary sources are not measured. Neither the 2023 TP gradient nor a 2022→2023 change can be interpreted as an increase caused by new wastewater in 2022.
- This does NOT use original Lycaenidae insect counts, adult puddling behavior, plant host records or biological ecological outcomes. Preserve original positive `chocho` 22/25 potential larval host resource geographic expansion separately.
