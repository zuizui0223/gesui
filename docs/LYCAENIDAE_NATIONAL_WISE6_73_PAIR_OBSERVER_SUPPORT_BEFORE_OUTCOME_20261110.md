Germany is the only country with original unequivocal registered qualifying WWTP source-zero comparison pairs. Freeze **ALL 73 Germany original directed monitoring pairs**: 33 original EEA both-N/P-positive reported facility freshwater outlet/source-interposed internal river paths, 40 no qualifying registered-interposed WWTP and no unresolved same-reach order, plus original 100 distinct monitor site IDs/coordinates.

The prior 8-quartet WISE6 local PR #35 involved 32 of these 100 stations; flag them, and report a new-only sensitivity. The original third-party converted HydroRIVERS topology is source-hash-frozen but not independently verified against official HydroRIVERS. Registered source-zero is not pristine or even definitively untreated river water.

Geographic overlap is intrinsically limited: at original country×2° blocks there are 12 blocks but only four containing both WWTP source statuses; there are **at most 7 matched four-arm source/control pairs**, before allowing for station reuse, photo effort, physical circle overlap, or observation effects. It is mathematically impossible to construct eight same-2° source/control quartets with these sites. A 3° bin sensitivity has seven blocks and only four with both classes, theoretical maximum 18 before any sampling exclusions. The 3° option is not site-level causal control.
