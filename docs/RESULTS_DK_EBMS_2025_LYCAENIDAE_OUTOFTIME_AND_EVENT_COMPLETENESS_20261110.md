# 2025 Denmark eBMS Lycaenidae out-of-time check: near-source lower in levels, 2024 depression reverses

**2026-11-10 — OUT-OF-TIME follow-up with source-only panel frozen before new 2025 species counts, but overall hypothesis explicitly POSTHOC after seeing 2022 DE/AT photographs and 2023/24 Denmark standardized counts.** These are *licensed survey-recorded butterfly individuals*, NOT true abundance censuses or wastewater exposure experiments.

- Original Denmark eBMS Darwin Core Archive v1.4, GBIF dataset caa38f94-ddeb-43e7-9a27-1ac46d7ddc5c, CC-BY-NC 4.0, exact original ZIP SHA256 `2cc64ec352754223db06a92eef5b01803968eac94bae34057c17f1abdb6da79b`. Original source 15,606 Event rows, 21,617 Occurrence rows.
- Original frozen 2023/24 **107 monitoring-site coordinate groups** (at least two distinct recorded visit days in both preceding years; explicit source location uncertainty <=500m) all have at least two source Event visit days in 2025. Counts and original 2025 source-only eligibility were SHA-committed **before** this 2025 individualCount read. The same original 2022 EEA qualifying Danish freshwater outfall inventory, 203 registered outfalls, is a time-invariant proximity covariate in 2023/24/25.
- Original source distances: **23 site rows <=5km** from qualifying 2022 registered source, **38 >=10km**, **46 intermediate**. Near 23 rows represent just FOUR distinct registered source outfall IDs (4,7,5,7 sites); no valid <=2km source-near study. Original 214 site-year 2023/24 family counts and original day totals were independently reproduced before opening the new 2025 family outcomes.
- Original 2025 near sites 23 have **198 original distinct Event survey dates**, 2025 remote 38 have **577**. These are NOT confirmed 198 and 577 independently complete butterfly censuses. Only 113/198 near and 298/577 remote site-dates have at least one published linked Occurrence; missing Occurrence extension is ambiguous as to genuine no butterflies versus unreported data.

## 1. Prespecified 2025 check on the original fixed 107-site event-denominator cohort

| Original full-year 2025 | <=5km registered WWTP | >=10km from qualifying WWTP |
|---|---:|---:|
| Original site-coordinate rows | 23 | 38 |
| Original distinct Event dates | 198 | 577 |
| Recorded Lycaenidae individuals | **95** | **585** |
| Lycaenidae per original Event date | **0.480** | **1.014** |
| Recorded Pieridae individuals | 148 | 324 |
| Recorded Lycaenidae three-family fraction | **95/243 = 39.1%** | **585/909 = 64.4%** |

**Precommitted directional out-of-time check PASSED**: 2025 near rate and three-family proportion were both lower than remote in pooled observations. This does NOT establish a WWTP-caused deficit, nor a generally independent replication across sources: only FOUR qualifying near-source groups exist.

**Countervailing temporal result:** unlike 2023 -> 2024 decline, 2024 -> 2025 original Event-date-normalized Lycaenidae count rate **rebounded more strongly near**: near 2025/2024 ratio **3.09**, remote **1.27**, relative rate-ratio **2.44**. The year 2024 low is not a monotonic negative wastewater trend, and 2022 effluent source annual P/N is not time-specific.

### Four source groups and single-year completeness

- 2025 original recorded Lycaenidae counts across four near outfall groups: **3, 0, 78, 14**. Thus **78/95 = 82.1%** of near-recorded Lycaenidae came from ONE registered source group, showing extreme leverage. The zero-count seven-site group had **28 original Event rows and ZERO published Occurrence rows** in 2025: its zeros are not established biological absence.
- Original 2025 Event/Occurrence ID-only audit: **215 near Event rows, 113 with any linked Occurrence (52.6%)** versus **706 remote Event rows, 301 linked (42.6%)**. Event row count is not the same as distinct visit dates, and higher near reporting linkage does not remove localized publication gaps or environmental confounding.
- Year-by-year whole-source visit-linked-QC results are archived. Strong evidence of missingness heterogeneity means the primary original-Event-denominator comparison is labeled descriptive, not a precise detection-adjusted butterfly abundance estimate.

## 2. Posthoc same original sites with >=2 linked butterfly/biota observation dates in BOTH 2024 and 2025

To avoid silently interpreting Event-only records without any published Occurrence as verified zeros, a more conservative **posthoc ascertainment sensitivity** selects identical sites with >=2 original site-dates/year linked to *any* published taxon occurrence. This is a biased subset toward reported biota and cannot be called an independently predeclared control.

| Original same-site published Occurrence coverage (2024 -> 2025) | <=5km near | >=10km remote |
|---|---:|---:|
| Eligible identical site rows | **14** | **27** |
| 2024 Lycaenidae / linked days | 34 / 112 = 0.304 | 494 / 300 = 1.647 |
| 2025 Lycaenidae / linked days | **95 / 112 = 0.848** | **585 / 292 = 2.003** |
| 2025/2024 linked-date rate ratio | **2.794** | **1.217** |

Relative rate-of-rate ratio near/remote = **2.297**. The rebound from the 2024 low persists, while 2025 near level remains below remote (0.848 vs 2.003). The eligible 14 near sites are clustered at only **THREE outfall groups**; the original registered seven-site group with no source-linked 2025 individual records is excluded. No valid four-source independent inference can result.

An even stricter **2023–2025 all-three-year identical-site** linked-2+ date sample retains near 10 sites and remote 26, originating from only **TWO** near registered outfalls. Near annual accepted Lycaenidae counts / linked days 2023 **74/58**, 2024 **30/91**, 2025 **92/94**; remote 2023 **385/247**, 2024 **494/295**, 2025 **584/285**. This is an apparent **V-shaped observation pattern**, but only two source-proximity units remain and the 2023-to-2024 recovery contrast cannot be generalized.

## 3. Stronger same-site and same-calendar-month linkage

The original licensed full Event/Occurrence archive was revisited with a strict **same site AND same calendar month with >=1 original Event carrying ANY published Occurrence in each of 2024 and 2025**. Denominator is the distinct source date having any published occurrence, not the raw Event date count. This is deliberately posthoc after the 2025 outcome: reporting coverage can itself depend on butterflies and other biota, so do not treat it as an unbiased detection correction.

| Same 2024 and 2025 observed months | <=5km near | >=10km remote |
|---|---:|---:|
| Identical qualifying site rows | **15** | **28** |
| 2024 Lycaenidae / documented date | **32/97 = 0.330** | **489/280 = 1.746** |
| 2025 Lycaenidae / documented date | **95/105 = 0.905** | **579/275 = 2.105** |
| 2025/2024 change in count rate | **2.743** | **1.206** |

**Relative year-rate ratio near / remote: 2.275.** Restricting the *same rule* to May–August yields **2.185**. This does not make 15 near sites into 15 independent WWTPs: only THREE registered source groups have documented 2025 records in this matched cohort. Leave-one-outfall-out descriptive relative rate-ratio range **1.965–2.426**, still above 1 but unsuitable as a source-randomized test.

If 2023 is ALSO required to have a linked published observation in the same calendar month, there are 11 near and 27 remote public site rows and only TWO near registered outfall groups. The 2024->2025 relative rate-ratio is 3.843, but original 2023/24 near-source occupation and linked-day coverage are too uneven for source-level significance.

## Scientific inference and next evidence threshold

**Replicable observation-only conclusion**: The fixed 2022 source proximity proxy predicts *lower 2025 recorded Lycaenidae rates and relative counts near the four original source groups* than in nonrandom remote Danish sites. At the same time, the *near-source recorded Lycaenidae rate rebounds in 2025* relative to its own anomalously low 2024 year, even under matched sites, linked individual-record dates, same-calendar-months and leave-one-source checks.

**Not warranted:** 'WWTP phosphorus causes a decline', 'sewage improved Lycaenidae recovery', 'a broad European sewage butterfly ecological law', 'adult protein puddling' or any historical WWTP treatment effect. The WWTP reporting year is static 2022 for all butterfly years; receptor chemistry, larval hosts, ant mutualists, landcover, within-month phenology, rainfall and source-specific geography are not controlled. Only 3 source systems have recorded Lycaenidae in 2025, and original source-site lack of occurrence data could be genuine absence or reporting non-completion. Neither a significant negative nor positive causal WWTP effect has been identified.

**Stop retuning radius, year and source-only survey filters in this small Denmark cohort.** For new ecological evidence, replicate standardized Lycaenidae records at at least 10 independently characterized receiving-water outfall systems with upstream/actual downstream transport, measured dissolved N/Na/organic substrates, larval host supply and independent sampling coverage; an alternative credible study could estimate time-specific effluent infrastructure changes with source-level staggered introduction/upgrade and controls. Keep this Denmark dataset as a bounded external temporal/photo-method falsification and sampling-process case study.
